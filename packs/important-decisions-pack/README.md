# Important Decisions Pack

**10 practical AI skills for founders and managers facing a choice that matters.** For agencies and professional-services teams of roughly 20–200, adaptable elsewhere.

Turn an unclear choice into real alternatives, visible trade-offs, honest uncertainty, and a decision someone can own. AI helps prepare and challenge your reasoning; you verify the facts and make the choice.

## Start in five minutes

1. Pick one real decision and describe your first view, even if it is only a hunch.
2. Paste `portable/decision-frame.md` into your assistant, or choose the skill matching your current need below.
3. Add the decision deadline, owner, constraints, and available evidence. Use aliases for people and clients.
4. Correct the draft and take the next useful step. You do not need to run the whole pack.

## The skills

| Skill | When to use it | You leave with |
|---|---|---|
| [Frame the Decision](skills/decision-frame/SKILL.md) | We need to decide whether to expand, but keep talking past each other. | A one-page decision frame. |
| [Create Real Alternatives](skills/decision-options/SKILL.md) | I can only see two options and neither looks good. | A shortlist of genuinely different options. |
| [Compare Trade-offs](skills/decision-tradeoffs/SKILL.md) | Help us compare options without a made-up score. | A consequence and trade-off table. |
| [Check the Outside View](skills/decision-uncertainty/SKILL.md) | Our forecast is mostly a story about why this project is different. | An evidence and uncertainty brief. |
| [Test the Preferred Option](skills/decision-counterevidence/SKILL.md) | I want to make sure I am not just proving my preferred answer. | A disconfirming-evidence plan. |
| [Gather Independent Input](skills/decision-independent-input/SKILL.md) | Everyone agreed after the founder spoke, and I am not sure we heard the facts. | A consultation and synthesis plan. |
| [Run a Grounded Premortem](skills/decision-premortem/SKILL.md) | Before we commit, help us spot how this could fail. | A risk and prevention note. |
| [Find What Would Change the Choice](skills/decision-sensitivity/SKILL.md) | Which uncertainty should we resolve before deciding? | A sensitivity and next-evidence brief. |

| [Review Without Hindsight](skills/decision-review/SKILL.md) | The result was poor. Was the decision bad or did something else happen? | A decision learning and revisit note. |

## Use with your assistant

**Any text assistant:** paste one `portable/<skill-name>.md` prompt into a new conversation. The prompts contain complete instructions and need no external files. Compatibility means readable instructions, not guaranteed identical behavior across models.

**A Project:** add `portable/ALL-SKILLS.md` as a source and paste `PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.md` into project instructions. Start a chat with the skill name. If retrieval is unreliable, paste that individual prompt instead.

**An Agent Skills workspace:** copy selected folders from `skills/` into the workspace’s skills directory according to your assistant’s documented setup. Each folder is standalone. Marketplace installation becomes available after publication; this review copy is not yet registered.

## Inside the pack

- `skills/`: 10 standalone skills.
- `portable/`: individual prompts and the combined `ALL-SKILLS.md`.
- `resources/evidence-and-sources.md`: academic evidence, counterevidence, and limits.
- `resources/practice-lab.md`: three fictional worked decisions with expected checks.
- `templates/decision-brief.md` and `templates/decision-review.md`: reusable blanks.
- `LICENSE.md`: Polar Bear’s internal/client-use license, not for resale.

## Practical limits

These workflows are original practice tools, not an experimentally validated intervention. Research on targeted decision exercises does not establish that a short AI prompt improves real organizational outcomes. The assistant cannot know your business from a thin description, determine another person’s motives, or produce reliable probabilities without evidence. It does not make employment selections, send messages, spend money, or replace qualified advice for regulated decisions.

A useful decision record can show both a defensible choice with a bad outcome and weak reasoning that happened to succeed. Review both the process and the result.
