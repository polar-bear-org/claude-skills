# Pricing Under AI Pressure: 29 Claude Skills

29 Claude skills for pricing your expertise when your clients have AI too, from your own numbers to the price increase letter. For founders and owners who sell their expertise, consultants, advisers and fractional leaders who set their own prices.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/ai-pricing-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/ai-pricing-pack)

## What this is

The skills follow the work in order. You start by knowing your numbers: the floor below which a job costs you money, which clients carry your margin, where AI actually saves time in your work and what it costs you. Then you decide what AI changes, productise an offer, price it on value and outcomes, prepare the price conversation (including the AI discount and "AI could do this in 10 seconds"), put your AI use and your terms in writing, and keep retainers, scope and prices current. Each skill produces one artifact and hands over to the next.

The red line: Claude works from your own numbers and helps you prepare an honest conversation; it never sets your price, never invents a benchmark or a competitor's rate, never hides your use of AI, and you have the conversation yourself.

```
Know your numbers -> Decide what AI changes -> Productise an offer -> Price on value and outcomes -> Have the price conversation -> Disclose AI and agree the terms -> Retainers, scope and raising prices
```

## The skills

### 1 · Know your numbers

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Floor Rate Calculator](skills/price-floor-rate/SKILL.md) | Fully loaded cost, real non-billable time, floor per day and per hour, kept internal | You are about to quote and do not know the number below which you lose money |
| [Client Profitability Map](skills/price-client-profitability/SKILL.md) | Margin per client, the whale curve, realisation by client, two clients to look at first | Margins look fine overall and you suspect one or two clients eat them |
| [AI Activity Map](skills/price-ai-activity-map/SKILL.md) | Activities per service, time before and after AI measured on real jobs, what stays human, net saving | A client asks for an AI discount and you cannot say where AI saves time |
| [AI Cost Ledger](skills/price-ai-cost-ledger/SKILL.md) | AI tool and API costs per client or offer, review time AI adds, absorb, bill or price in | The AI bills grow and you do not know whether to bill or absorb them |

### 2 · Decide what AI changes

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [AI Savings Decision](skills/price-ai-savings-decision/SKILL.md) | Pass on, keep as margin or reinvest, per service, with the line you will say to clients | You ask whether to pass AI savings on or keep them as margin |
| [Full-Price Work List](skills/price-full-price-list/SKILL.md) | What clients pay for beyond the output, your evidence, what no longer earns full price | You ask what still earns full price when AI did most of the work |
| [AI Positioning Statement](skills/price-ai-positioning-statement/SKILL.md) | How you use AI in plain words, what clients get that their own AI does not give them, short versions | Clients think AI is good enough and you do not know how to position |
| [Pricing Models Comparison](skills/price-pricing-models-comparison/SKILL.md) | Hourly to outcome models side by side on five considerations, who carries the risk, your pick | You ask how to bill when AI does the work |

### 3 · Productise an offer

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Productized Service Sheet](skills/price-productized-service/SKILL.md) | One named offer with outcome, fixed scope, timeline, fixed price from your numbers | Every project is custom-quoted and the price follows the hours |
| [Service Blueprint](skills/price-service-blueprint/SKILL.md) | The offer from the client's side, where AI works and where a named person checks and decides | You need to show a client honestly what AI does and what you do |
| [Offer Unit Economics](skills/price-offer-unit-economics/SKILL.md) | Price, delivery and AI cost per sale, contribution margin, sales needed to break even | You have an offer and do not know how many you must sell |
| [AI Workflow Handover Offer](skills/price-ai-workflow-offer/SKILL.md) | The options when a client wants to buy your AI workflow, IP and licensing questions to check with a qualified adviser | Clients ask to buy your AI workflow instead of hiring you |

### 4 · Price on value and outcomes

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Value Discovery Questions](skills/price-value-discovery-questions/SKILL.md) | Questions on the outcome, its worth in the client's numbers, the cost of doing nothing, the alternative | You want to price on value and do not know what the work is worth to this client |
| [Value-Based Pricing Worksheet](skills/price-value-based-pricing/SKILL.md) | Value drivers in the client's numbers, value range, your floor, the price you set and why | You are moving from hours to value and need a number you can explain |
| [Outcome-Based Pricing Plan](skills/price-outcome-based-pricing/SKILL.md) | Outcome metric, baseline, attribution rule, base fee plus outcome part, cap, floor, window | A client wants to pay for results and you must check the outcome is fair to measure |
| [Three-Option Proposal](skills/price-three-option-proposal/SKILL.md) | One to three real options by scope and outcome, fee by phase, assumptions, plain terms, dated next steps | You send one take-it-or-leave-it number and the client can only say cheaper |

### 5 · Have the price conversation

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Price Conversation Prep Sheet](skills/price-conversation-prep/SKILL.md) | Your number, your walk-away floor, your alternative, their interests, first two sentences, trades | The price call is tomorrow and you tend to discount before anyone asks |
| [AI Objection Answer](skills/price-ai-objection-answer/SKILL.md) | What is true in the objection, what clients get from you, an offer to show your workflow, your reply | A client says "AI could do this in 10 seconds" |
| [AI Discount Response](skills/price-ai-discount-response/SKILL.md) | What the request asks, what changed in the work, four honest options, the trades, a reply draft | A client asks for a discount because you use AI now |
| [AI-Written Brief Review](skills/price-ai-brief-review/SKILL.md) | The brief reduced to one objective and audience, gaps and invented facts, questions back, scope impact | The client's long AI-written brief says everything and nothing |
| [Client AI Feedback Triage](skills/price-client-ai-feedback/SKILL.md) | A chatbot review sorted against the agreed brief, change requests, a calm reply, the cost of a new round | The client ran your work through a chatbot and says you are doing a bad job |

### 6 · Disclose AI and agree the terms

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [AI Disclosure Policy](skills/price-ai-disclosure-policy/SKILL.md) | What AI does and what a person always does, client data rules, how clients are told, opt-out | You wonder whether to tell clients you use AI |
| [Statement of Work](skills/price-statement-of-work/SKILL.md) | Outcome, deliverables, acceptance criteria, assumptions, revision rounds, change triggers, AI use line | The price is agreed and the scope must stop moving |
| [AI Contract Questions](skills/price-ai-contract-questions/SKILL.md) | Grouped questions for your adviser on AI clauses, with your facts attached | A client sends an agreement with new AI clauses |

### 7 · Retainers, scope and raising prices

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Retainer Redesign](skills/price-retainer-redesign/SKILL.md) | What the retainer buys in hours and in value, the options, the move per client, the conversation outline | Your retainer is sold as hours and AI has made the hours shrink |
| [Scope Change Request](skills/price-change-request/SKILL.md) | The ask, impact against the signed scope, accept, defer, swap or decline, the change log entry | "It's just one small change", again |
| [Price Increase Plan](skills/price-increase-plan/SKILL.md) | Which clients and offers, the new price from your numbers, timing, notice, order, your decision per client | You want to raise prices and keep putting it off |
| [Price Increase Letter](skills/price-increase-letter/SKILL.md) | A short plain letter per client with the new price, date, notice, an honest line on how you work now | The increase is decided and you need the words |
| [Quarterly Pricing Review](skills/price-pricing-review/SKILL.md) | Floor re-run, margins, AI savings and costs, fee leakage, wins and losses on price, three decisions | Pricing only gets looked at when a client complains |

## Example prompts

- "Run price-floor-rate. Here are my monthly costs and the days I actually billed last quarter."
- "A client wants [X]% off because I use AI now. Here is their email and my AI Activity Map. Help me prepare my answer."
- "My client said AI could do this in 10 seconds. Help me answer honestly, without selling against AI."
- "Should I pass the time AI saves me on to clients or keep it as margin? Here are my numbers per service."
- "Turn my most common project into a productised offer with a fixed price from my own costs."
- "I want to raise prices for three clients next quarter. Help me plan it and write the letters for me to send."

## Quality bar

- Your numbers only: every cost, rate, saving and price comes from what you give; anything missing stays a [bracketed placeholder].
- No invented benchmarks, market rates or competitor prices, and no invented client quotes or results.
- No manipulative pricing: no decoy options, false scarcity, deadline pressure or anchoring tricks; three options only when each is a real offer.
- Honest about AI: what AI does and what a person decides is said plainly, and nothing sells against AI.
- Every output ends with the decision you make, and you send or say everything yourself.
- Contracts, IP, tax, data protection and disclosure law are written as questions with "check with a qualified adviser", never as advice.
- The red line: Claude works from your own numbers and helps you prepare an honest conversation; it never sets your price, never invents a benchmark or a competitor's rate, never hides your use of AI, and you have the conversation yourself.
