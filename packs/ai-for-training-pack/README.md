# AI for L&D and Corporate Training: 30 Claude Skills

30 Claude skills for L&D work, from the first training request to proof of what changed. For L&D leads, instructional designers and corporate trainers.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/ai-for-training-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/ai-for-training-pack)

## What this is

The skills follow the job in the order it happens. You find the real need before anyone says "course", get the know-how out of the experts, design the programme, build the materials, deliver it live, make it stick back at work, prove what changed, and run the L&D desk with the time and budget you actually have. Each skill is one named method or one artifact, with a fixed output you can hand to a sponsor, an SME or a trainer.

The red line: Claude drafts the learning and an expert signs it off; people learn by practising with other people, and no quiz, survey or role-play is ever used to grade, rank or build a case against a person.

```
Find the real need -> Get it out of the experts -> Design the programme -> Build the materials -> Deliver it live -> Make it stick -> Prove what changed -> Run the L&D desk
```

## Install

### In Claude Code

```
/plugin marketplace add polar-bear-org/claude-skills
/plugin install ai-for-training-pack@polar-bear-skills
```

### In Claude.ai

1. Turn on "Code execution and file creation" under Settings > Capabilities.
2. Go to Customize > Skills, click "+", choose "+ Create skill", then "Upload a skill", and upload the skill's zip from the `install/` folder.
3. On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner enables "Cloud code execution and file creation" and "Skills" under Organization settings > Plugins & skills (Policy tab), and can upload skills for the whole organization.

## The skills

### 1 · Find the real need

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Training Request Intake](skills/ld-training-request-intake/SKILL.md) | Intake questions, the problem in one line, an "is it training?" triage, a reply to the requester | "We need a training by Friday" lands with the answer already chosen |
| [Performance Gap Analysis](skills/ld-performance-gap-analysis/SKILL.md) | Desired versus actual performance, a cause table, non-training options, a recommendation | The manager, process or tools may be the cause, not a skill gap |
| [Training Needs Assessment](skills/ld-training-needs-assessment/SKILL.md) | Needs by role and task, evidence for each, a priority table, what training cannot fix | You need what a team actually lacks, not a yearly wish list |
| [Action Map](skills/ld-action-map/SKILL.md) | One measurable goal, observable actions, why they are not happening, practice, minimum information | The sponsor wants a course and you need "they will do", not "they should know" |

### 2 · Get it out of the experts

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [SME Interview Guide](skills/ld-sme-interview-guide/SKILL.md) | Incident questions, decision points, common mistakes, exceptions, scenario seeds | The expert gives you the standard workflow but solves real cases by intuition |
| [Content Triage](skills/ld-content-triage/SKILL.md) | Must-do, must-know, look-up and cut table, job aid candidates, a cut list with reasons | The SME says learners need to know everything and sends the whole slide deck |
| [SME Review Plan](skills/ld-sme-review-plan/SKILL.md) | Who reviews what, one decider, rounds and dates, a feedback log, a change rule | Six departments review one course and the revisions never end |

### 3 · Design the programme

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Learning Objectives](skills/ld-learning-objectives/SKILL.md) | Observable objectives at the right Bloom's level, conditions and criteria, rewrites | The only objective is "understand the topic" |
| [Instructional Design Document](skills/ld-design-document/SKILL.md) | Audience, need, objectives, strategy, practice and checks, evaluation, decisions, sign-off | Nobody sees the design decisions behind a course |
| [Curriculum Map](skills/ld-curriculum-map/SKILL.md) | Modules by objective, activity, check and format, sequence, learning path | A programme has grown module by module and nothing connects |

### 4 · Build the materials

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Scenario-Based Learning](skills/ld-scenario-based-learning/SKILL.md) | Decision scenarios from real mistakes, branches and consequences, feedback per choice | People finished every module and still cannot make the call on the job |
| [E-Learning Storyboard](skills/ld-elearning-storyboard/SKILL.md) | Screen-by-screen text, visuals, interactions, narration, accessibility notes | The build starts from slides and ends as a knowledge dump with voiceover |
| [Knowledge Check](skills/ld-knowledge-check/SKILL.md) | Scenario questions per objective, distractors from real mistakes, feedback, retake rules | An AI can answer your quiz from a screenshot and it proves nothing |
| [Job Aid](skills/ld-job-aid/SKILL.md) | A checklist, decision table or one-page flow, where it lives, who updates it | People need the answer mid-task, not a 30-minute module |
| [AI Content QA Checklist](skills/ld-ai-content-qa/SKILL.md) | Source-traced accuracy, alignment, accessibility, myth and bias scan, SME sign-off record | AI drafted the course, AI reviewed it, and no human checked it teaches anything |

### 5 · Deliver it live

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Session Plan](skills/ld-session-plan/SKILL.md) | A timed flow (open, recall, show, practise, apply), activities, materials, the first five minutes | A virtual session competes with email and chat from minute one |
| [Facilitator Guide](skills/ld-facilitator-guide/SKILL.md) | Cues per segment, questions to ask, activity instructions, timing flex, what-if notes | Trainers are told to read the SOP aloud from slides |
| [Role-Play Training](skills/ld-role-play-training/SKILL.md) | Role cards, learning points, good and poor models, private observer notes, debrief | People sit through role-play, meet real customers, and you hope it stuck |
| [Train-the-Trainer Kit](skills/ld-train-the-trainer/SKILL.md) | Teach-back plan, practice rounds, delivery notes, a readiness conversation guide | The programme depends on facilitators who each need to learn it first |

### 6 · Make it stick

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [70-20-10 Plan](skills/ld-70-20-10-plan/SKILL.md) | On-the-job assignments, coaching and peer learning, the formal pieces | The programme is all classroom and nothing happens back at work |
| [Spaced Repetition Plan](skills/ld-spaced-repetition-plan/SKILL.md) | Retrieval prompts at widening gaps, 5-minute micro-units, schedule and channel | People forget a 60-minute course within weeks |
| [Manager Follow-Up Kit](skills/ld-manager-follow-up-kit/SKILL.md) | Before and after conversation guides, the first on-the-job task, check-ins, a barrier log | Learners go back to a manager who never asks what they learned |
| [Onboarding Training Plan](skills/ld-onboarding-training-plan/SKILL.md) | A week-by-week path, the real tasks to handle, buddy sessions, sign-off by doing | Ramp-up drags on and stakeholders blame the talent |

### 7 · Prove what changed

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Kirkpatrick Evaluation Plan](skills/ld-kirkpatrick-evaluation-plan/SKILL.md) | Level 4 result and leading indicators, Level 3 behaviours and drivers, Level 2 and 1 checks | Leadership sees a high completion rate and moves on |
| [Training Evaluation Form](skills/ld-training-evaluation-form/SKILL.md) | Questions on relevance, confidence to apply and barriers, open prompts, a summary template | The smile sheet says everyone loved it and tells you nothing |
| [Success Case Study](skills/ld-success-case-study/SKILL.md) | A short survey on use, an interview guide, anonymous case write-ups, a barrier list | You need evidence of use on the job, not more survey scores |
| [Training ROI Estimate](skills/ld-training-roi/SKILL.md) | Benefits and how they were isolated, money conversion, full costs, a conservative ROI range | The budget holder asks what the programme returned |

### 8 · Run the L&D desk

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Training Plan](skills/ld-training-plan/SKILL.md) | Programmes ranked against business goals, capacity and budget, a quarterly calendar, a not-this-year list | One person must build two programmes at once |
| [ADDIE Project Plan](skills/ld-addie-project-plan/SKILL.md) | Phases, deliverables, owners, review gates, realistic durations, SAM iterations | The brief lands Thursday and the programme is due Monday |
| [Compliance Training](skills/ld-compliance-training/SKILL.md) | The few decisions per rule, scenario checks, a refresh cadence, limits on the record | Compliance is a box to tick and quiz records are kept as evidence against people |

```

Need objectives you can observe?                  -> Learning Objectives

Need modules that connect?                        -> Curriculum Map
Need practice that looks like the job?            -> Scenario-Based Learning
Need an e-learning build brief?                   -> E-Learning Storyboard
Need a quiz that checks decisions?                -> Knowledge Check
Need help at the moment of the task?              -> Job Aid
Need to check AI-drafted content?                 -> AI Content QA Checklist
Need a live session that holds attention?         -> Session Plan
Need a guide another trainer can run?             -> Facilitator Guide
Need role-play that transfers?                    -> Role-Play Training
Need facilitators ready to deliver?               -> Train-the-Trainer Kit
Need learning back at work, not only class?       -> 70-20-10 Plan
Need people to remember it in a month?            -> Spaced Repetition Plan
Need managers to follow up?                       -> Manager Follow-Up Kit
Need new hires ready for real tasks?              -> Onboarding Training Plan
Need an evaluation plan before launch?            -> Kirkpatrick Evaluation Plan
Need a better end-of-course form?                 -> Training Evaluation Form
Need stories of use on the job?                   -> Success Case Study
Need a return figure the budget holder trusts?    -> Training ROI Estimate
Need the year's priorities on one page?           -> Training Plan
Need a project plan with real dates?              -> ADDIE Project Plan
Need compliance training that changes choices?    -> Compliance Training
```

## Example prompts

- "Run ld-training-request-intake. The sales director wants a negotiation course by Friday; here is the email."
- "Here are my notes from the service team lead. Build a performance gap analysis and tell me if training is the fix."
- "Our SME sent the attached slide deck. Run ld-content-triage and give me a cut list he can accept."
- "Turn these three objectives into a scenario-based learning module with branches built from the mistakes in my SME notes."
- "Plan the evaluation for the new supervisor programme, starting from the Level 4 result the sponsor cares about."
- "I have one quarter, one designer and these six requests. Build the training plan and the not-this-year list."

## Quality bar

- Every skill asks what you have first, and works from a minimum when you have little, marking the result as a first draft.
- Every method is named and attributed to its originator through a public source, and its real mechanics drive the steps.
- Every output has a fixed shape and ends with the decision a named person makes, and by when.
- No invented numbers: thresholds, costs and targets stay as [placeholders] until you set them.
- Legal and regulatory points are marked "check with a qualified adviser", never stated as advice.
- The red line: Claude drafts the learning and an expert signs it off; people learn by practising with other people, and no quiz, survey or role-play is ever used to grade, rank or build a case against a person.

## Where it comes from

This pack is research-informed. The methods come from their originators' public pages, institution guides and peer-reviewed papers on retrieval, spacing, transfer, behaviour modelling and cognitive load; the struggles come from a scan of what L&D people say about their work. Sources, their limits and which skill uses each are in `resources/evidence-and-sources.md`. Cited sources do not imply endorsement. This is a practice resource, not a validated intervention.
