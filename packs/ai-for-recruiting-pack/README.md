# AI for Recruiting: 31 Claude Skills

31 Claude skills for recruiting from the intake meeting to the signed offer, in-house and on the agency desk. For recruiters, talent acquisition partners, sourcers and agency recruiters.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/ai-for-recruiting-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/ai-for-recruiting-pack)

## What this is

The pack follows a search from start to finish. You brief the role with the hiring manager, keep candidates informed at every stage, attract the right applicants, source and reach out, screen and interview on the same criteria for everyone, then decide and close the offer. Agency recruiters get a desk of their own for winning clients, agreeing terms, presenting candidates and reporting on a search. The last domain runs the desk: the numbers, the rules for AI, and the interviewers.

Claude writes the search, the questions and the message, never the verdict: it does not screen, rank or score a candidate, and a person reads every application and makes every hiring decision.

```
Brief the role -> Keep candidates in the loop -> Attract -> Source and reach out -> Screen and interview -> Decide and close -> Agency desk -> Run the desk
```

## The skills

### 1 · Brief the role

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Intake Meeting](skills/recruit-intake-meeting/SKILL.md) | Question plan, profile calibration, must-have versus trainable list, one-page intake summary | The manager cannot describe the job and your search drifts |
| [Job Scorecard](skills/recruit-job-scorecard/SKILL.md) | First-year outcomes, 4 to 6 criteria with evidence, where each is assessed | Two jobs hide in one title and "senior" means five things |
| [Salary Range Brief](skills/recruit-salary-range/SKILL.md) | Proposed range from your evidence, where it appears, candidate lines, adviser questions | You fear "we didn't budget for this" after four rounds |
| [Hiring Process Plan](skills/recruit-hiring-process/SKILL.md) | Stage map, maximum rounds, feedback deadlines, decision meeting date | There is always one more interview and the pipeline goes cold |

### 2 · Keep candidates in the loop

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Candidate Communication Plan](skills/recruit-candidate-communication-plan/SKILL.md) | A promise per stage, the "no update yet" message, close-the-loop rule, keep-warm notes | Candidates hear nothing for weeks and the good ones go silent |
| [Interview Scheduling Messages](skills/recruit-interview-scheduling/SKILL.md) | Invitation with adjustments offer, confirmation, reminder, reschedule, apologies | A screen was cancelled twelve minutes before it started |
| [Rejection Email](skills/recruit-rejection-email/SKILL.md) | Messages per stage, job-related feedback lines, finalist call script, calm reply | You have decided no and need to say it kindly and fast |
| [Candidate Experience Survey](skills/recruit-candidate-experience-survey/SKILL.md) | 5 to 7 questions per stage, send timing, aggregate reading, fix list | Strong candidates decline because of how the process felt |

### 3 · Attract

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Job Description](skills/recruit-job-description/SKILL.md) | Outcomes-first description, must-haves only, pay and adjustments lines, wording check | You are about to repost last year's description |
| [Job Ad](skills/recruit-job-ad/SKILL.md) | Hook, the real job with its hard parts, pay, rules up front, what happens next | Hundreds apply who ignored the rules, and the right people scroll past |
| [Employee Referral Program](skills/recruit-employee-referral-program/SKILL.md) | The ask to staff, good referral guide, bonus rules as placeholders, update loop | Inbound is full of fakes and you want warm introductions |

### 4 · Source and reach out

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Boolean Search Strings](skills/recruit-boolean-search/SKILL.md) | Synonym sets, strings for LinkedIn, X-ray and boards, broad and narrow versions, test log | Your strings return thousands or nobody |
| [Talent Mapping](skills/recruit-talent-map/SKILL.md) | Target companies and teams, adjacent titles, feeder fields, pool estimate | The manager wants "our exact industry" and you need evidence |
| [Sourcing Strategy](skills/recruit-sourcing-strategy/SKILL.md) | Channel mix, free routes, weekly targets, what to stop | Inbound is unusable and replies have collapsed |
| [Recruiting Email Templates](skills/recruit-recruiting-email-templates/SKILL.md) | First message and two follow-ups from real facts, subject lines, InMail version | Everyone can spot a message pasted from a chatbot |

### 5 · Screen and interview

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Screening Questions](skills/recruit-screening-questions/SKILL.md) | Evidence questions for the form, phone screen guide, work authorisation step for a person | Hundreds of applications a week and a person still reads each one |
| [STAR Interview Questions](skills/recruit-star-interview-questions/SKILL.md) | Behavioural questions per criterion, STAR probes, situational questions, questions never to ask | Candidates answer "tell me about a time" with what they would do |
| [Structured Interview](skills/recruit-structured-interview/SKILL.md) | Interview plan, who covers what, order, timing, note rules, adjustments | Every interviewer asks their favourite questions |
| [Work Sample Test](skills/recruit-work-sample-test/SKILL.md) | A short task that mirrors the job, time limit, instructions, evidence guide | CVs no longer tell you who can do the work |
| [Interview Scorecard](skills/recruit-interview-scorecard/SKILL.md) | Blank form per interviewer, anchored evidence levels, notes space, no total | You need evidence written down before anyone says "I liked her" |

### 6 · Decide and close

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Interview Debrief](skills/recruit-interview-debrief/SKILL.md) | Agenda that collects scorecards first, criterion by criterion, decision record | The loudest voice decides, or the manager asks to "see a few more" |
| [Reference Check Questions](skills/recruit-reference-check/SKILL.md) | Consent step, questions tied to criteria, probes, never-ask list, note template | You want references that tell you something |
| [Offer Letter](skills/recruit-offer-letter/SKILL.md) | Letter from approved terms, conditions, offer call script, clause list for an adviser | The offer is approved and must go out today |
| [Counter Offer Plan](skills/recruit-counter-offer/SKILL.md) | Pre-offer questions, resignation prep, honest counter-offer answers, keep-warm plan | Declines and counter-offers keep taking your accepted candidates |

### 7 · Agency desk

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Recruiter Business Development](skills/recruit-business-development/SKILL.md) | Account list from hiring signals, one-page approach, first-call questions, four-touch plan | Mass outreach to decision makers is dead |
| [Agency Terms of Business](skills/recruit-terms-of-business/SKILL.md) | Plain explainer of fees, guarantee, introduction period, payment, adviser checklist | Clients ignore fees or hire someone you introduced |
| [Candidate Submittal](skills/recruit-candidate-submittal/SKILL.md) | Consent record, facts against each criterion, pay, notice, availability, no fit score | You want the client to read evidence, not adjectives |
| [Client Search Update](skills/recruit-client-search-update/SKILL.md) | Weekly activity, market feedback, proposed brief change, decisions by a date | The client goes quiet, chases daily, or rejects everyone |

### 8 · Run the desk

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Recruiting Metrics](skills/recruit-recruiting-metrics/SKILL.md) | Time to fill and hire, source, acceptance, pass-through, ageing with reasons | You are judged on roles that were dead on arrival |
| [AI Recruiting Policy](skills/recruit-ai-recruiting-policy/SKILL.md) | Allowed and banned uses, candidate notices, human review steps, adviser questions | A vendor promises AI screening and you need a line to defend |
| [Interviewer Training](skills/recruit-interviewer-training/SKILL.md) | 45-minute session plan and a one-page cheat sheet | Interviewers improvise and the debrief has no evidence |

## Example prompts

- "Run recruit-intake-meeting. Here are my notes from a 20-minute call with the hiring manager and three profiles she liked. Build the question plan for our follow-up."
- "Turn this job scorecard into a job description and flag any wording that could put people off."
- "I need Boolean strings for a backend engineer who knows payments. No paid tools, so include Google X-ray versions."
- "Plan the interview loop for this role: four interviewers, two rounds at most, a decision meeting date, and a blank scorecard for each interviewer."
- "My candidate just got a counter-offer. Help me prepare an honest conversation and a keep-warm plan for the notice period."
- "Draft this week's client search update. The client has turned down every candidate, and here are the reasons they gave."

## Quality bar

- Every skill starts from the job: outcomes and criteria agreed with the hiring manager, not a copied description.
- Every candidate meets the same questions, the same stages and the same messages.
- Every output has a fixed shape, a finish line, and a named person who decides next and by when.
- Messages use only facts you supply. Nothing is scraped, invented or guessed about a person.
- Legal and regulatory points (pay transparency, work authorisation, automated decision rules, agency terms) are flagged: check with a qualified adviser.
- Claude writes the search, the questions and the message, never the verdict: it does not screen, rank or score a candidate, and a person reads every application and makes every hiring decision.
