# Claude for Marketing Graduates: 31 Claude Skills

31 Claude skills for doing the junior marketing job well with Claude, then showing it, from first brief to first job. For marketing graduates, final-year marketing students, business graduates heading into marketing, marketing assistants and graduate marketing executives.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/grad-marketing-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/grad-marketing-pack)

## What this is

The pack follows the junior marketing job in the order the work arrives, then helps you show it. It starts by setting up AI-fluent work: a brand voice guide, reusable prompt briefs, a review of every AI draft and a check of every claim. Then it researches the audience and market from public evidence, plans the campaign (creative brief, messaging house, campaign plan, launch checklist), creates content in the brand voice across social, short video, email, landing pages and SEO, and reports and improves: metrics read in plain words, a monthly report with the bottom line first, and a test plan written before the test. The last two groups turn that work into evidence: a proof project on a real brand, a portfolio case study, an honest note on how you used AI, and then the application and interview. Each skill is one named method or one artifact, with the inputs it needs, where it breaks, a fixed output template, a finish line and the next skill to run.

Every skill holds one line: Claude helps you prepare, practise, research, organise and critique your own work. It never sits a test, interview or assessment for you, never invents experience, and never writes assessed work unless the rules allow it and you say so. In marketing work it never invents a statistic, a result, a review, a testimonial or a customer quote: every claim in your copy traces to evidence someone holds.

```
Set up AI-fluent marketing work -> Research the audience and market -> Plan the campaign -> Create content in the brand voice -> Report and improve -> Build a proof project -> Apply and interview
```

## The skills

### 1 · Set up AI-fluent marketing work

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Brand Voice Guide](skills/gmkt-brand-voice-guide/SKILL.md) | "We are / we are not" attributes, tone by situation, word list, three before-and-after rewrites, Project instructions block | You write for a brand that is not yours and every draft sounds like a different person |
| [Marketing Prompt Brief](skills/gmkt-prompt-brief/SKILL.md) | Reusable brief per recurring task, three starters, what never goes into a prompt | Your AI drafts come back generic because the prompt was one line |
| [AI Draft Review](skills/gmkt-ai-draft-review/SKILL.md) | Line-by-line check for facts, voice, generic tells and unsourced numbers, kept / changed / cut log, sign-off line | The draft reads fine and nobody has actually checked it |
| [Claim Substantiation Check](skills/gmkt-claim-check/SKILL.md) | Every claim listed, evidence held or missing, reviews and ad labels checked, rewrite or cut, questions for an adviser | The copy says "best" or a percentage and nobody knows where it came from |

### 2 · Research the audience and market

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Audience Persona](skills/gmkt-audience-persona/SKILL.md) | One or two personas from public evidence, each line cited, assumptions marked, three things to test | The brief says "millennials" and nobody has read what customers write |
| [Jobs to Be Done Map](skills/gmkt-jobs-to-be-done/SKILL.md) | Job statements, push, pull, anxiety and habit from real reviews, the message each force suggests | You know who buys but not why they switched or hesitate |
| [Competitor Content Audit](skills/gmkt-competitor-content-audit/SKILL.md) | Channels, themes, formats and rhythm, claims sorted into table stakes, rare and specific, gaps; a map, never a ranking | Your manager asks what competitors are doing on social by Friday |
| [SWOT Analysis](skills/gmkt-swot-analysis/SKILL.md) | SWOT with a source per line, PESTLE prompts, paired factors turned into actions | Your SWOT is four lists with no "so what" |

### 3 · Plan the campaign

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Creative Brief](skills/gmkt-creative-brief/SKILL.md) | Objective, audience, single-minded proposition, reason to believe, mandatories, deliverables, measures, deadline | The designer or agency needs a brief and you have a meeting note |
| [Messaging House](skills/gmkt-messaging-house/SKILL.md) | Umbrella message, three pillars, sourced proof points, words to avoid, one line per channel | Every channel says something slightly different about the same launch |
| [Campaign Plan](skills/gmkt-campaign-plan/SKILL.md) | Situation, SMART objectives with a baseline, strategy, tactics by channel, actions with owners, control | You are asked to "pull together a plan" for a launch or event |
| [See Think Do Care Plan](skills/gmkt-see-think-do-care/SKILL.md) | Audience at each stage, content and channel per stage, one metric per stage, gaps in the mix | All the content talks to people ready to buy and nobody else |
| [Campaign Launch Checklist](skills/gmkt-launch-checklist/SKILL.md) | UTM naming sheet, link and tracking tests, named approvals, ad labels, alt text, go or no-go owner | Launch is tomorrow and the links, tags and approvals are unchecked |

### 4 · Create content in the brand voice

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Content Calendar](skills/gmkt-content-calendar/SKILL.md) | Month grid by channel, pillar, format, owner, status and approval date, evidence behind each post | You post when there is time and the month has no shape |
| [Social Media Posts](skills/gmkt-social-media-posts/SKILL.md) | One idea as LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok variants, three hooks, alt text, "Ad" label where needed | You need this week's posts in the brand voice and they all sound like AI |
| [Short Video Script](skills/gmkt-short-video-script/SKILL.md) | Opening hook, beat-by-beat script, shot list, on-screen text and captions, edit notes | The team wants a reel and you have a phone and an afternoon |
| [Email Newsletter](skills/gmkt-email-newsletter/SKILL.md) | Three subject lines with preview text, body, one call to action, plain-text version, a subject line test | The newsletter is due and last month's got few opens |
| [Landing Page Copy](skills/gmkt-landing-page-copy/SKILL.md) | Hero line, problem, benefits, sourced proof, objections and FAQ, call to action, claim list | A campaign needs a page and you have a product sheet |
| [SEO Content Brief](skills/gmkt-seo-content-brief/SKILL.md) | Target query, search intent, outline from real questions, title and meta description, internal links, draft | You are asked for "a blog post for SEO" and have a topic, not a query |

## Quality bar

- **One method, applied properly.** Each skill uses the real mechanics of its source (the tone dimensions, the SOSTAC stages, the See Think Do Care stages, the UTM fields, the GA4 definitions), not a generic "gather, analyse, recommend".
- **Evidence before claims.** Every number, proof point, persona line and competitor claim comes with where it came from. Anything without a source is marked as an assumption or left as a [placeholder].
- **A person checks every draft.** AI drafts go through a review that records what a person checked, kept, changed and cut before anything is sent or published.
- **The brand's voice, the graduate's own voice.** Brand copy follows the brand's voice guide; applications, posts and interview answers stay in your own words, from your own experience.
- **Work is scored, people are not.** Skills score evidence, options, test ideas and fit to a brief, never a person, a competitor's staff or a candidate. Advertising, consumer law and data protection points end with "check with a qualified adviser".
- **The red line:** Claude helps you prepare, practise, research, organise and critique your own work. It never sits a test, interview or assessment for you, never invents experience, and never writes assessed work unless the rules allow it and you say so. In marketing work it never invents a statistic, a result, a review, a testimonial or a customer quote: every claim in your copy traces to evidence someone holds.

## Where it comes from

The pack is research-informed. The roster comes from the tasks listed in UK graduate and junior marketing job ads (reporting on performance, social media, web and SEO copy, email, assets, campaign support), from what marketing graduates say they struggle with, and from surveys of marketing leaders on AI and early careers. Each method is attributed to its originator through a public source: the Advertising Standards Authority and the Competition and Markets Authority, Google's Analytics Help and Search Central, Nielsen Norman Group, the Clayton Christensen Institute, CIPD, the IPA, Anthropic's documentation and AI Fluency course, W3C, and UK careers guidance. Sources and their limits are in `resources/evidence-and-sources.md`.

Cited sources do not imply endorsement. This is a practice resource, not a validated intervention.
