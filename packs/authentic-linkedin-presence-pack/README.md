# Claude Playbook for Authentic LinkedIn Presence: 29 Claude Skills

29 Claude skills for a LinkedIn presence built from your own ideas, in your own voice, that starts client conversations. For founders, owners, partners, consultants, speakers and authors.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/authentic-linkedin-presence-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/authentic-linkedin-presence-pack)

## What this is

A weekly system along one arc. You start with your point of view: who you help, the situations they are in when they need you, and what you believe. Your profile then tells a buyer why to talk to you. Claude helps you find the ideas already sitting in your calls, client questions and talks, shapes them in your own voice, checks every claim, and turns them into a few honest formats. Then the work moves to comments and conversations with people you choose, and a weekly rhythm that measures conversations started, not likes.

The red line: Claude finds the idea in your own work and checks the draft against your own voice; it never invents a story, client, number or opinion, and it never posts, comments or messages for you.

```
Your point of view -> Profile that sells -> Find the ideas -> Write in your voice -> Formats -> LinkedIn networking: comments and conversations -> Weekly rhythm and what to measure
```

## The skills

### 1 · Your point of view

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Ideal Client Profile](skills/flink-ideal-client-profile/SKILL.md) | Situations your best clients were in, the job they hired you for, their words, who you are not for | You post about everything because you never wrote down who you help |
| [Buying Situations Map](skills/flink-buying-situations-map/SKILL.md) | Moments a buyer starts needing you, the ones you can speak to, one post theme each | You want to come to mind when the need arrives, months before anyone shops |
| [Point of View Statement](skills/flink-point-of-view/SKILL.md) | Three beliefs, the shift behind each, evidence from your work, where you might be wrong | You have opinions in meetings and none on LinkedIn |
| [Positioning Statement](skills/flink-positioning-statement/SKILL.md) | One sentence in three versions, the project behind each, words to drop | Your answer to "what do you do?" changes every time |

### 2 · Profile that sells

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [LinkedIn Headline](skills/flink-linkedin-headline/SKILL.md) | Five headline options, buyer search words, the read-aloud test | Your headline is a job title and buyers cannot tell why to talk to you |
| [LinkedIn About Section](skills/flink-linkedin-about-section/SKILL.md) | First two lines, the problem, how you work, two proofs, how to start a conversation | Your About reads like a CV |
| [LinkedIn Featured Section](skills/flink-featured-section/SKILL.md) | Three to five items chosen, one line each, what to make when one is missing | A referred buyer lands on your profile and finds nothing to read |
| [LinkedIn Recommendation Request](skills/flink-recommendation-request/SKILL.md) | Who you choose to ask, a short ask naming the project, a reminder of the work | Clients who loved your work never said so in public |

### 3 · Find the ideas

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Founder Interview](skills/flink-founder-interview/SKILL.md) | Questions about your week, your answers word for word, two ideas worth a post | You sit down to post and have nothing to say |
| [Client Questions Log](skills/flink-client-questions-log/SKILL.md) | Questions clients actually asked, the question behind each, which deserve a public answer | You answer the same question in every call and never in public |
| [Content Repurposing Plan](skills/flink-content-repurposing/SKILL.md) | Separate ideas inside a talk or article, one post per idea, publishing order | You gave a good talk and nothing came of it online |
| [Idea Bank](skills/flink-idea-bank/SKILL.md) | One idea per line with its source, buying situation, format and status | Ideas come in calls and are gone by Monday |

### 4 · Write in your voice

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Personal Voice Guide](skills/flink-voice-guide/SKILL.md) | Tone dimensions, "I am X but not Y" lines, your words, words you never use, rhythm | AI drafts sound like everyone and you cannot say what "like me" means |
| [Voice Memo to Post](skills/flink-voice-memo-to-post/SKILL.md) | A post from your own spoken sentences, every added word marked | You think out loud far better than you write |
| [AI Tells Check](skills/flink-ai-tells-check/SKILL.md) | AI tells flagged by type, read-aloud test, a suggested cut for each flag | The draft is fine and does not sound like you |
| [Claim and Permission Check](skills/flink-claim-check/SKILL.md) | Every story, number and quote traced to a source, client permission recorded, do-not-claim list | The post names a project, a result or a client line |
| [AI Use Statement](skills/flink-ai-use-statement/SKILL.md) | How you use Claude in your writing, as one line and one paragraph | Someone asks "did AI write this?" and you want an honest answer ready |

### 5 · Formats

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Project Story Post](skills/flink-project-story-post/SKILL.md) | One moment from a real project, what you did, an ending that is a plain fact | You did good work and nobody outside the room knows |
| [Lesson Learned Post](skills/flink-lesson-learned-post/SKILL.md) | What you planned, what happened, why, what you do differently now | Your most useful lesson this quarter came from a mistake |
| [Opinion Post](skills/flink-opinion-post/SKILL.md) | A dated news hook, your thesis, three points, the best counterargument | You disagree with advice your clients keep hearing |
| [LinkedIn Carousel](skills/flink-linkedin-carousel/SKILL.md) | Six to ten text slides, one idea each, exported as a PDF document post | A framework you use with clients deserves a version people can save |

### 6 · LinkedIn networking: comments and conversations

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Warm Conversations List](skills/flink-warm-conversations-list/SKILL.md) | Up to ten people you choose, how well you know each in your words, the reason to talk | People react to your posts and you never follow up |
| [Thoughtful Comment](skills/flink-thoughtful-comment/SKILL.md) | Two or three angles for a post you chose, the facts you would add | "Great post" says nothing and you want to be known |
| [Connection Request Message](skills/flink-connection-request/SKILL.md) | One short note per person you met, the real context, no pitch | You met people this week and a blank request will be ignored |
| [Conversation Opener](skills/flink-conversation-opener/SKILL.md) | The reason to write, two or three points, an easy way to decline | A good comment thread ended at "thanks" |

### 7 · Weekly rhythm and what to measure

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Content Calendar](skills/flink-content-calendar/SKILL.md) | The month at a glance, one to three posts a week, the hours it costs, a rule for skipped weeks | You post in bursts, then disappear for a month |
| [Weekly LinkedIn Routine](skills/flink-weekly-linkedin-routine/SKILL.md) | A 60 minute checklist, what Claude prepares, what only you do | LinkedIn falls off the week first |
| [Conversations Log](skills/flink-conversations-log/SKILL.md) | Each conversation started, where it came from, the next step and date | You cannot tell whether LinkedIn brings work |
| [Monthly LinkedIn Review](skills/flink-monthly-review/SKILL.md) | What started conversations, what stayed silent, stop, start and continue, one change | A month has passed and you are about to judge it by likes |

## Example prompts

- "Interview me about my week, one question at a time, and find two ideas worth a post."
- "Here are 15 of my old posts and emails. Build my personal voice guide."
- "Check this draft against my voice guide and flag the AI tells. Do not rewrite it."
- "This post names a client project. Trace every claim and number to my notes."
- "Turn this transcript of my talk into a repurposing plan, one post per idea."
- "From my comments this month, help me pick who I would like to talk to and why."

## Quality bar

- Every idea, story and number comes from your own work, with its source named.
- Drafts keep your words; anything Claude adds is marked for you to keep or cut.
- Client details stay out unless the client agreed, for this channel.
- Nothing scores, ranks or profiles people; you decide who to talk to and how well you know them.
- No automation, scraping, pods or engagement bait, in line with LinkedIn's own rules.
- Success is conversations started, logged by you, not likes or reach.
- The red line: Claude finds the idea in your own work and checks the draft against your own voice; it never invents a story, client, number or opinion, and it never posts, comments or messages for you.
