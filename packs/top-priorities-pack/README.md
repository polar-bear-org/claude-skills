# How to Make Time for Your Top Priorities

**10 practical AI skills for founders and managers whose important work keeps losing space to everything else.** Built for agencies and professional-services teams of roughly 20–200, and adaptable elsewhere.

Choose meaningful outcomes, see what fits, protect useful working time and renegotiate what cannot fit. You leave with decisions, calendar proposals, messages and realistic plans. Your judgment and real conversations remain central.

## Start in five minutes

Choose one situation below. Paste its complete `portable/` prompt into your assistant, then describe a real example using aliases. Start with `priorities-capacity-plan` if the week is already overloaded; start with `priorities-choose-outcomes` if you cannot say what deserves the space.

Example: “I have 35 working hours next week, 15 hours of fixed delivery, 4 hours of coordination, and 20–26 hours of new work. Help me make a plan with explicit trade-offs.”

## Use with your assistant

For one conversation, paste an individual file from `portable/`. For a project, add `portable/ALL-SKILLS.md` as a source and paste `PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.md` into its instructions. If retrieval misses the chosen section, paste the individual prompt directly. All prompts are self-contained; no external research access is required to run them. Results vary by assistant and context window.

For a local Agent Skills setup, copy selected folders from `skills/` into the location supported by your assistant, such as `.claude/skills/` for a Claude Code project. Individual upload ZIPs, when included in the reviewed download, contain standalone skills. Marketplace installation becomes available after publication; this review copy does not claim to be registered.

## Inside the pack

- `skills/`: ten standalone workflows.
- `portable/`: individual complete prompts and `ALL-SKILLS.md`.
- `PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.md`: routing and human-review guidance.
- `resources/evidence-and-sources.md`: 13 academic sources, evidence types and limits.
- `resources/practice-lab.md`: fictional worked cases, including a low-control service role.
- `templates/`: reusable capacity, trade-off and review notes.
- `LICENSE.md`: internal and client use; not for resale.

## Where judgment stays human

The skills draft, organize and challenge. They do not send messages, cancel meetings, set someone else’s workload or decide whose personal commitments count. A plan that requires chronic overtime is a workload decision awaiting resolution. Essential service work, care, breaks and relationships belong in the calculation.

This pack focuses on allocating your own work. Identifying delegation as an option does not replace a separate human agreement about ownership, support and the other person’s capacity.

The workflows are original practice designs informed by academic research; the pack itself has not been tested as an intervention. Research includes modest average effects, observational confounds and limited transfer from laboratory tasks. See the evidence notes before using research claims in a workshop.
