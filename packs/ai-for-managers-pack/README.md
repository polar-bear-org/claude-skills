# AI for Managers: 31 Claude Skills

31 Claude skills for running a team, from the first 1:1 to the hard conversation. For people managers and team leads, first-time and experienced.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/ai-for-managers-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/ai-for-managers-pack)

## What this is

The skills follow a manager's real week, starting where it hurts most. You prepare the conversation you keep putting off, step into the role, delegate and let go, protect the team's capacity, manage up and through change, run the team's rhythm, set direction, and coach and grow your people. Each skill is one named method or one artifact you walk away with: a prep sheet, a 1:1 agenda, a delegation board, a capacity case, a decision record, a development plan.

The red line: Claude prepares, you have the conversation: no skill judges, scores or ranks a person, and nothing written about someone leaves your hands unread.

```
Have the hard conversation -> Step into the role -> Delegate and let go -> Protect the team's capacity -> Manage up and through change -> Run the rhythm -> Set direction -> Coach and grow
```

## Install

### In Claude Code

```
/plugin marketplace add polar-bear-org/claude-skills
/plugin install ai-for-managers-pack@polar-bear-skills
```

### In Claude.ai

1. Turn on "Code execution and file creation" under Settings > Capabilities.
2. Go to Customize > Skills, click "+", choose "+ Create skill", then "Upload a skill", and upload the skill's zip from the `install/` folder. Repeat for each skill you want.
3. On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner enables "Cloud code execution and file creation" and "Skills" under Organization settings > Plugins & skills (Policy tab), and can upload skills for the whole organization.

## The skills

### 1 · Have the hard conversation

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Difficult Conversation Prep](skills/mgr-difficult-conversation-prep/SKILL.md) | Purpose line, dated facts, opening sentences, likely reactions, follow-up note | The conversation you keep putting off |
| [SBI Feedback Model](skills/mgr-sbi-feedback/SKILL.md) | Situation, behaviour and impact statements, intent question, one request | Feedback lands wrong, or people hear only the criticism |
| [Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)](skills/mgr-performance-improvement-plan/SKILL.md) | Expected standard, dated gap, support offered, review dates, questions for HR | Someone is not doing the job and you need a fair path |
| [Conflict Resolution](skills/mgr-conflict-resolution/SKILL.md) | Issue map, each side's interests, options, agreed behaviours | Two people cannot work together |
| [Return-to-Work Conversation](skills/mgr-return-to-work-conversation/SKILL.md) | Welcome plan, questions, adjustments to consider, agreed note | Someone is back after absence |

### 2 · Step into the role

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [New Manager 30-60-90 Day Plan](skills/mgr-new-manager-plan/SKILL.md) | Listening plan, map of the work, first decisions, 90-day check | Promoted with no training, or taking over a team |
| [One-on-One Meeting Agenda](skills/mgr-one-on-one-agenda/SKILL.md) | First 1:1 questions, report-owned agenda, question bank, actions | You manage former peers, or 1:1s drift into status updates |
| [Manager README](skills/mgr-manager-readme/SKILL.md) | How I work, what I expect, how to disagree with me | The team does not yet know how you will manage |

### 3 · Delegate and let go

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Levels of Delegation](skills/mgr-delegation-levels/SKILL.md) | Decision areas on seven levels, the board, changes to announce | People ask your approval for everything |
| [Delegation Handoff Note](skills/mgr-delegation-handoff/SKILL.md) | Outcome, limits, authority level, check-ins, what done looks like | Handing over work so it does not come back up |
| [RACI Matrix](skills/mgr-raci-matrix/SKILL.md) | Tasks against roles, one Accountable per row, gaps flagged | Nobody knows who owns what |
| [Cross-Training Plan](skills/mgr-cross-training-plan/SKILL.md) | Critical tasks, cover today, learning pairs, what to document | The team falls apart when one person is away |

### 4 · Protect the team's capacity

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Team Capacity Planning](skills/mgr-capacity-check/SKILL.md) | Work inventory, real hours, stop, delay or ask list, case for leadership | The team is overloaded and you cannot shield them |
| [Eisenhower Matrix](skills/mgr-eisenhower-matrix/SKILL.md) | Your week in do, schedule, delegate, drop, three priorities | You are running on empty and everything feels urgent |
| [Team Psychological Safety Check](skills/mgr-psychological-safety-check/SKILL.md) | Anonymous team questions, discussion guide, one team experiment | People stopped speaking up |
| [Team AI Playbook](skills/mgr-ai-team-playbook/SKILL.md) | Agreed uses, never-put-in list, review rules, review date | AI arrived with a usage target and no team rules |

### 5 · Manage up and through change

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Managing Up Brief](skills/mgr-managing-up-brief/SKILL.md) | One-page situation, background, assessment, recommendation, the ask | You are caught between leadership and your team |
| [Skip-Level Meeting](skills/mgr-skip-level-meeting/SKILL.md) | Agenda, question set, themes without names, follow-up | You run a skip-level or prepare for one |
| [Change Announcement](skills/mgr-change-announcement/SKILL.md) | What is decided, what is not, what changes for whom, FAQ | You deliver a decision you did not make |
| [DACI Decision](skills/mgr-daci-decision/SKILL.md) | Driver, approver, contributors, informed, options, decision record | Decisions stall or get made above your head |

### 6 · Run the rhythm

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Team Meeting Agenda](skills/mgr-team-meeting-agenda/SKILL.md) | Purpose, timed items, decisions needed, action log | Team meetings turn into an hour of monologue |
| [Working Agreements](skills/mgr-working-agreements/SKILL.md) | Channels, hours, response times, escalation path, review date | Nobody agreed how the team works |
| [Team Retrospective (Start, Stop, Continue)](skills/mgr-team-retrospective/SKILL.md) | Facilitation plan, prompts, themes, owned actions | The same problems keep coming back |

### 7 · Set direction

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Team Charter](skills/mgr-team-charter/SKILL.md) | Purpose, scope, who we serve, goals, roles, decision rights | Nobody wrote down what good looks like |
| [Team OKRs](skills/mgr-team-okrs/SKILL.md) | One to three objectives, measurable key results, check-in rhythm | The team works hard on the wrong things |
| [SMART Goals](skills/mgr-smart-goals/SKILL.md) | Goals rewritten as SMART, linked to team OKRs, check dates | Expectations for one person are vague |

### 8 · Coach and grow

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [GROW Coaching Model](skills/mgr-grow-coaching/SKILL.md) | Goal, reality, options and will questions, the person's next step | You jump in with answers and people stop bringing problems |
| [Career Conversation](skills/mgr-career-conversation/SKILL.md) | Question guide, energises and drains, options map | People want growth you cannot map |
| [Individual Development Plan (IDP)](skills/mgr-individual-development-plan/SKILL.md) | One or two goals, experiences, learning, support, dates | A career talk needs to turn into action |
| [Stay Interview](skills/mgr-stay-interview/SKILL.md) | Question set, listening notes, a stay plan you own | Good people leave and you did not see it coming |
| [Employee Recognition Note](skills/mgr-recognition-note/SKILL.md) | A specific note, where to share it, a team-wide list | People feel unseen |

## Example prompts

- "Run mgr-difficult-conversation-prep. I need to tell a report that their deadlines keep slipping. Here are my notes from the last three weeks."
- "I just got promoted and now manage four people who were my peers last month. Build my first 1:1 questions."
- "My team is drowning. Here is our work list and who is on leave. Help me build a case for my director on what to stop."
- "Leadership decided we are moving to a new rota and I disagree. Draft the announcement and the FAQ for my team."
- "Turn my messy notes from a career talk into an individual development plan my report can edit."
- "Put our recurring decisions on a delegation board. I sign off on everything and it is killing me."

## Quality bar

Every skill in this pack:

- Uses one named method or one artifact, with the method's real steps, not generic advice.
- Tells you exactly what to paste, and starts from the minimum when you have little.
- Says when not to use it, and what fits better.
- Ends with a fixed template and a decision a named person makes, by a date.
- Invents no numbers, quotes or findings, and sends legal and health points to a qualified adviser or HR.
- Holds the red line: Claude prepares, you have the conversation: no skill judges, scores or ranks a person, and nothing written about someone leaves your hands unread.
