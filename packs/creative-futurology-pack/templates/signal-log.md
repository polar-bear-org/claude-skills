## How to fill a card

- Title: what happened, in one line, as a fact. "Regulator X published draft rules on Y" beats "Regulation is coming".
- Date: when it happened or was published, not when you saw it.
- Source: a link or a document name a colleague could open. If the source is a conversation, write "conversation with [role], [date]" and mark the card as hearsay.
- What it is: one or two sentences describing the thing itself.
- Why it might matter for the focal question: one or two sentences. Guessing is fine here, and is labeled as a guess by the field name.
- Territory: social, technological, economic, environmental, or political. Pick one, the main one.
- Strength: weak (one instance, easy to dismiss), emerging (a few instances, a pattern is possible), or established (widely reported, already shaping decisions).
- Spotted by: first name or role. Never a judgment about the person, only a name so the scanner can ask a follow-up question.
