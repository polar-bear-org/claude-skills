# Failures and Mistakes Pack

**Eight practical AI skills to contain mistakes, take responsibility, investigate conditions, and test prevention.** For founders and managers in agencies and professional-services teams of roughly 20–200.

Use a real incident or near miss. Leave with a response, timeline, repair plan, or tested change. This pack addresses a specific failure; use a team-performance pack for recurring retrospectives and working agreements.

## Start in five minutes
1. For ongoing harm, begin with **Contain the Incident** and use the responsible incident route.
2. Otherwise choose the job below and paste its matching `portable/` file into your assistant.
3. Describe the situation using aliases, known facts, and your initial view.
4. Verify the draft and carry out the real conversation or action yourself.

## Skills

| Skill | Use it when | Output |
|---|---|---|

| [Reconstruct the Event](skills/failure-reconstruct-the-event/SKILL.md) | Accounts conflict or hindsight is replacing evidence after an error. | An evidence-labeled timeline and targeted questions. |
| [Map Contributing Conditions](skills/failure-map-contributing-conditions/SKILL.md) | The explanation has stopped at carelessness, poor communication, or one root cause. | A multi-factor explanation and testable prevention hypotheses. |
| [Respond to a Reported Error](skills/failure-respond-to-a-reported-error/SKILL.md) | Someone tells you about a mistake or near miss and your response will affect future reporting. | A first response and a clear reporting follow-through note. |

| [Test a Prevention Change](skills/failure-test-a-prevention-change/SKILL.md) | A review produced recommendations but no evidence that they work. | A small prevention test with monitoring and rollback. |
| [Close the Incident and Transfer Learning](skills/failure-close-and-transfer-learning/SKILL.md) | Repairs are complete but lessons may be forgotten or copied too widely. | A closure record and one reusable, bounded learning note. |

## Use the pack
Individual prompts in `portable/` are self-contained plain-text instructions for assistants that accept them. For a project, add `portable/ALL-SKILLS.md` as reference material and use `PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.md` as instructions. If retrieval fails, paste the individual prompt directly. Model context limits vary.

For an Agent Skills-compatible tool, copy selected folders under `skills/` into its configured skills directory. Marketplace installation becomes available after publication; this review draft does not claim registration.

## Human judgment
AI prepares drafts. It does not adjudicate fault, guarantee confidentiality, diagnose people, determine formal consequences, or execute incident actions. Use authorized human routes for safety, security, regulated, or formal employment concerns. Do not upload unnecessary client or employee records.

The pack is research-informed, not an evaluated intervention. Containment and repair can be urgent; learning must not delay them. A report completed is not evidence that a failure has been prevented.
