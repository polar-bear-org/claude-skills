# The Complete Claude Guide for Product Managers: 42 Claude Skills

42 Claude skills for the whole product job, stage by stage, from the vision to the weekly update. For product managers, product owners and heads of product.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/complete-claude-guide-for-product-managers-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/complete-claude-guide-for-product-managers-pack)

## What this is

The guide follows the product job in the order you do it. It sets direction (vision, strategy, impact map, market size, lean canvas, north star, roadmap), goes out to customers (interviews, synthesis, personas, journey, jobs, value proposition, opportunity tree), decides what to build and what to refuse (triage, RICE, ICE, WSJF, cost of delay, business case, DACI, decision log), defines the work (one-pager, PR/FAQ, prototype brief, PRD, AI feature spec), delivers it (story map, user stories, sprint planning, sprint review, MoSCoW), launches it (competitive analysis, go-to-market, pre-mortem, release notes) and proves whether it worked (success metrics, experiments, post-launch review). Prove loops back to Discover. Around every stage sits the rhythm of running the product: the weekly update, the retro and the quarter plan. Each skill is named as an action and keeps one framework inside, with the inputs it needs, where it breaks, a fixed output template, a finish line and the next skill to run. Where a skill fits a Claude surface (Claude Docs, Claude Slides, Claude Design, Projects, connectors, Claude in Chrome), it says so, and beta features are marked beta.

Every skill holds one line: Real customers are not optional: Claude drafts the documents, the team talks to users, and a named person makes the call.

```
Set direction -> Discover -> Decide -> Define -> Deliver -> Launch -> Prove -> Run the product
```

## The skills

### 1 · Set direction

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Write the Product Vision](skills/pmg-write-product-vision/SKILL.md) | Vision statement options, Product Vision Board (target group, needs, product, business goals) | The team cannot say in one sentence what it is building toward |
| [Write the Product Strategy](skills/pmg-write-product-strategy/SKILL.md) | Diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions, "we will not" list | Everything is high priority and leaders set the roadmap by preference |
| [Map the Impact](skills/pmg-map-the-impact/SKILL.md) | Goal, actors, impacts, deliverables, the deliverables no impact needs marked to cut | The roadmap is a feature list and nobody can say whose behaviour each item changes |
| [Size the Market (TAM, SAM, SOM)](skills/pmg-size-the-market/SKILL.md) | Top-down and bottom-up estimates, sourced assumptions, a range, sanity checks | Leadership asks how big this is and the slide needs a number you can defend |
| [Fill the Lean Canvas](skills/pmg-fill-lean-canvas/SKILL.md) | One-page business logic, riskiest assumptions marked untested | A new product or bet needs its business logic on one page |
| [Pick the North Star Metric](skills/pmg-pick-north-star-metric/SKILL.md) | North star, three to five named inputs with definitions, counter-metrics | The team ships a lot and cannot say which number it is moving |
| [Build the Product Roadmap](skills/pmg-build-product-roadmap/SKILL.md) | Now-next-later board, confidence notes, "not on the roadmap" list, one-page executive version | The roadmap is run from the CEO's inbox and every date becomes a promise |

### 2 · Discover

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Prepare the Customer Interview Guide](skills/pmg-prepare-interview-guide/SKILL.md) | Screener lines, story-based questions, probes, consent note, notes template | You have 30 minutes with a customer and cannot waste it on opinions |
| [Synthesise the Interviews](skills/pmg-synthesise-interviews/SKILL.md) | Affinity clusters, need statements with sources, contradictions, open questions | Five interview transcripts and a PRD due Friday |
| [Build the Personas](skills/pmg-build-personas/SKILL.md) | Composite personas from interview evidence, each claim linked to its source | The team argues about "the user" and everyone means someone different |
| [Map the Customer Journey](skills/pmg-map-customer-journey/SKILL.md) | Stages, actions, thoughts and feelings, pain points, opportunities, evidence per cell | Each team owns a slice and nobody sees where customers drop off |
| [Map the Jobs to Be Done](skills/pmg-map-jobs-to-be-done/SKILL.md) | Job statements, circumstances, forces for and against switching, alternatives | A request arrives with no known job behind it |
| [Fill the Value Proposition Canvas](skills/pmg-fill-value-proposition-canvas/SKILL.md) | Customer profile, value map, fit notes, one positioning line every team uses | Marketing, sales and product each describe the product differently |
| [Build the Opportunity Solution Tree](skills/pmg-build-opportunity-tree/SKILL.md) | Outcome, opportunities, target opportunity, three ideas, riskiest assumption per idea | Discovery keeps getting squeezed and has to fit between sprints |

### 3 · Decide

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Triage the Feature Requests](skills/pmg-triage-feature-requests/SKILL.md) | Request log, the job behind each request, build / later / no sort, reply drafts | Sales promised it, a big client wants it, the CEO forwarded it, all this week |
| [Score the Backlog with RICE](skills/pmg-score-with-rice/SKILL.md) | Scored backlog, confidence notes, out-of-order exceptions, cut list | 40 requests, room for 5, and the loudest voice keeps winning |
| [Score with ICE](skills/pmg-score-with-ice/SKILL.md) | Impact, confidence and ease scores, evidence behind each confidence score, top ten to test | Forty growth ideas and one afternoon to pick what to test |
| [Rank with WSJF](skills/pmg-rank-with-wsjf/SKILL.md) | Cost of delay parts divided by job size, ranked list, sensitivity check | Several teams share one backlog and need one order they can defend |
| [Cost the Delay](skills/pmg-cost-the-delay/SKILL.md) | Urgency profiles, CD3 order, "yes, if" options, reply to the asker | Everything is urgent and you need a no that holds |
| [Make the Business Case](skills/pmg-make-business-case/SKILL.md) | One-page case in five parts, assumptions table with ranges and owners, what would change the answer | You must argue for a feature in finance language and the ROI numbers are guesses |
| [Set Up the DACI Decision](skills/pmg-set-up-daci-decision/SKILL.md) | Driver, approver, contributors, informed, inputs, decision date | Sign-off never comes, or comes and is reversed next week |
| [Keep the Decision Log](skills/pmg-keep-decision-log/SKILL.md) | Dated records (context, decision, status, consequences), reopening rule | Settled calls keep coming back as if nobody decided |

### 4 · Define

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Write the One-Pager](skills/pmg-write-one-pager/SKILL.md) | Problem, who it is for, why now, the bet, success, what we will not do, the ask | An idea needs one page before anyone writes a PRD |
| [Write the PR/FAQ](skills/pmg-write-pr-faq/SKILL.md) | One-page press release, customer FAQ, internal FAQ | A prototype is already built and nobody wrote down why |
| [Write the Prototype Brief](skills/pmg-write-prototype-brief/SKILL.md) | The belief it tests, pass line set in advance, what it does not prove, gap-to-production list | Leadership saw the AI demo and thinks it ships next week |
| [Write the PRD](skills/pmg-write-the-prd/SKILL.md) | Problem and evidence, goals, non-goals, numbered testable requirements, open questions, success measures | The last spec was read five different ways, or the AI draft runs to 12 pages |
| [Spec the AI Feature](skills/pmg-spec-the-ai-feature/SKILL.md) | Task, measurable success criteria, acceptable error rate, eval set plan, human fallback, launch bar | Stakeholders expect it to be right every time, and one prompt fix breaks another case |

### 5 · Deliver

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Build the Story Map](skills/pmg-build-story-map/SKILL.md) | User activity backbone, walking skeleton, release slices | The backlog is 200 tickets and nobody can see the whole product |
| [Write the User Stories](skills/pmg-write-user-stories/SKILL.md) | Stories, Given / When / Then criteria, INVEST check, refinement questions | Engineers say the requirements are thin but not what is missing |
| [Plan the Sprint](skills/pmg-plan-the-sprint/SKILL.md) | Sprint goal as an outcome, capacity by role, items the team selected, definition of done check | The sprint is filled to a date promised upstairs and the team never sized the work |
| [Run the Sprint Review](skills/pmg-run-sprint-review/SKILL.md) | Increment against the sprint goal, feedback, backlog changes, short key-updates deck | The review is a demo nobody outside the team attends |
| [Cut Scope with MoSCoW](skills/pmg-cut-scope-with-moscow/SKILL.md) | Must / Should / Could / Won't list, Must budget check, Won't-this-time list | The date is fixed and scope has to give |

### 6 · Launch

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Write the Competitive Analysis](skills/pmg-write-competitive-analysis/SKILL.md) | Landscape table with sources, win / loss themes, where you differ, what not to copy | Sales keeps saying "the competitor has it" |
| [Plan the Go-to-Market](skills/pmg-plan-go-to-market/SKILL.md) | Launch tier, audiences, positioning line, readiness by team, enablement brief | The product is ready and sales, support and marketing are not |
| [Run the Pre-Mortem](skills/pmg-run-pre-mortem/SKILL.md) | Failure story, reasons list, top risks with owners and signals, go / adjust / stop | The launch date was promised before anyone asked what could go wrong |
| [Write the Release Notes](skills/pmg-write-release-notes/SKILL.md) | Customer notes by change type, internal version for support, who is affected | Support hears about changes from customers |

### 7 · Prove

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Set the Success Metrics](skills/pmg-set-success-metrics/SKILL.md) | HEART table (goals, signals, metrics), baseline, target, guardrail | You celebrated a release and still cannot say whether it worked |
| [Design the Experiment](skills/pmg-design-the-experiment/SKILL.md) | Hypothesis, variants, primary metric, guardrails, sample and duration check, decision rule | Two options, a strong opinion on each side, and enough users to test |
| [Run the Post-Launch Review](skills/pmg-run-post-launch-review/SKILL.md) | Intended versus actual, why, keep / change list, keep, fix or remove call | Two weeks after launch customers still use the old workflow |

### 8 · Run the product

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Write the Weekly Update](skills/pmg-write-weekly-update/SKILL.md) | News-first subject line, bottom line and asks, shipped, at risk, decisions by date, one cut per audience | Friday goes on an update people skim, then ask the same questions in three meetings |
| [Run the Retro](skills/pmg-run-the-retro/SKILL.md) | Last action checked, a format for this cycle, themes, one owned change with a check date | The retro raises the same problems every sprint and the actions belong to no one |
| [Plan the Quarter](skills/pmg-plan-the-quarter/SKILL.md) | Objectives and key results, capacity split, bets, what is out, check-in dates | The quarter starts, the goals are a feature list, and every date becomes a promise |

## Example prompts

- "Run pmg-triage-feature-requests. Here are this week's requests from sales, support and the CEO's forward. Sort them and draft the replies."
- "Leadership wants to know how big this market is by Thursday. Run pmg-size-the-market with these customer counts and price points, and show every assumption."
- "Here are five interview transcripts. Run pmg-synthesise-interviews and show me where customers contradict each other."
- "We are adding an AI summary to support tickets. Run pmg-spec-the-ai-feature: what does good look like, and who sets the error rate we can live with?"
- "Here are this week's shipped tickets and the two risks I know about. Run pmg-write-weekly-update with a version for leaders and one for sales and support."
- "Our last three retros all said 'improve communication'. Run pmg-run-the-retro and start from the action we never closed."

## Quality bar

- **One framework, applied properly.** Each skill uses the real mechanics of its method (the scales, the grid, the formula, the order of the pass), not a generic "gather, analyse, recommend".
- **It tells you where it breaks.** Every skill says when not to use it and which skill in the guide fits instead.
- **Same shape every time.** A fixed output template and a short "done when" check before handover.
- **No invented facts.** No made-up quotes, customers, market numbers, ROI figures or findings. Missing data becomes a bracketed placeholder or an open question.
- **Work is scored, people are not.** Skills score requests, ideas, options, risks and key results, never team members or individual users. Customer data is summarised as needs, and personas are composites, never profiles of real people.
- **The red line:** Real customers are not optional: Claude drafts the documents, the team talks to users, and a named person makes the call.
