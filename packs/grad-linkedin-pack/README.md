# Claude for LinkedIn Networking for Recent Graduates: 28 Claude Skills

28 Claude skills for an honest LinkedIn presence in your own voice, from an empty profile to a 20-minute weekly routine. For final-year students and new graduates.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/grad-linkedin-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/grad-linkedin-pack)

## What this is

The pack follows a graduate's first month on LinkedIn, then the weekly rhythm after it. It starts with your story: an interview that finds the real evidence in your modules, dissertation, placement, part-time jobs and societies, the roles you want next, your own voice and a straight answer on how you use AI. Then it builds the profile line by line from that evidence, turns one project into proof of work a recruiter can open, helps you post as a graduate without the cringe, comment and connect one person at a time, get found by recruiters and alumni safely, and keep it all alive in 20 minutes a week with a monthly refresh. Each skill is one named method or one artifact, with the inputs it needs, where it breaks, a fixed output template, a finish line and the next skill to run.

Every skill holds one line: You did the work; Claude helps you say it: it interviews you about what really happened and never adds a project, a number, a skill or a quote you cannot back up, it never touches your account (you write, post, comment and connect yourself, with no automation and no scraping), and it never sits a test, interview or assessment for you or writes assessed work unless your university's rules allow it and you say so.

```
Know your story -> Build the profile -> Show your proof of work -> Post as a graduate -> Comment and connect -> Be found by recruiters and alumni -> The weekly 20 minutes
```

## Install

### In Claude Code

```
/plugin marketplace add polar-bear-org/claude-skills
/plugin install grad-linkedin-pack@polar-bear-skills
```

### In Claude.ai

1. Turn on "Code execution and file creation" under Settings > Capabilities.
2. Go to Customize > Skills, click "+", choose "+ Create skill", then "Upload a skill", and upload the skill's zip from the `install/` folder. Repeat for each skill you want.
3. On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner first enables "Cloud code execution and file creation" and "Skills" under Organization settings > Plugins & skills (Policy tab), and can upload skills for the whole organization.

Every skill works in a normal chat with what you paste in. Some name an optional Claude surface: a Project (redesigned, beta, select plans) to keep your Experience Inventory and notes in one place, memory with editable Topics to hold your voice guide between chats, Claude Docs (beta) for the project case, and the desktop app's create to Google Sheets (Google Drive connected) for the activity log. Claude never opens, browses or acts on your LinkedIn account: you paste text in, and you do everything on LinkedIn yourself.

## The skills

### 1 · Know your story

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Experience Inventory](skills/glink-experience-inventory/SKILL.md) | Interview notes, one evidence row per real thing you did, strengths backed by two rows, gaps marked as gaps | You think you have no real experience and the profile looks empty |
| [Target Role Brief](skills/glink-target-role-brief/SKILL.md) | Two or three target roles, employer words from five real job ads, matching evidence rows, what to say yes to next | You are about to write a headline and cannot say what you want next |
| [Personal Voice Guide](skills/glink-personal-voice-guide/SKILL.md) | Your words and phrases, sentence length, words you never use, AI tells to strip, before and after lines | Every AI draft sounds like a stranger, and like everyone else's |
| [Personal AI Use Policy](skills/glink-ai-use-policy/SKILL.md) | One page on what you use Claude for and never do, how you disclose AI help, your check before anything goes public | You want a straight answer ready when an employer asks how you use AI |

### 2 · Build the profile

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [LinkedIn Headline](skills/glink-linkedin-headline/SKILL.md) | Three headline options within LinkedIn's limit, status plus target role plus proof, the evidence row behind each | Your headline says "Student at [university]" and nothing else |
| [LinkedIn About Section](skills/glink-about-section/SKILL.md) | One or two short paragraphs in your voice, every claim tied to an evidence row | The About box is empty, or full of "passionate, driven, motivated" |
| [Experience Entries](skills/glink-experience-entries/SKILL.md) | Two to four CAR lines per role, numbers only with a source, transferable skill per line | You think a bar job or a society role does not count |
| [Education and Projects Entries](skills/glink-education-projects/SKILL.md) | Degree entry with matching modules and dissertation, Projects entries with your part, links as media | Your strongest evidence is coursework and it sits as one line |
| [LinkedIn Skills Section](skills/glink-skills-section/SKILL.md) | Five to ten skills from your brief, each linked to proof, a "learning" list for the rest | You added 40 skills and none is the one the job ad asks for |
| [LinkedIn Profile Audit](skills/glink-profile-audit/SKILL.md) | Section-by-section check, truth check against your CV and evidence, fixes in order | The profile is "done" and you want to know what a reader sees first |

### 3 · Show your proof of work

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Project Interview](skills/glink-project-interview/SKILL.md) | Six-moment interview on one project, your words kept, a do-not-claim list | You did a real project and cannot get past "we got a good mark" |
| [Project Case Study](skills/glink-project-case/SKILL.md) | One-page case, a 150-word Featured version, the permission note | You want one thing a recruiter can open that shows how you think |
| [Featured Section Plan](skills/glink-featured-section/SKILL.md) | Three to five items to pin, a line for each, permission check, reading order | Featured is empty, or holds work you are not sure you may share |
| [LinkedIn Recommendation Request](skills/glink-recommendation-request/SKILL.md) | Who to ask, a short personal request with real reminders, a thank-you | You want a recommendation without the default request or writing it for them |

### 4 · Post as a graduate

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Post Ideas Bank](skills/glink-post-ideas-bank/SKILL.md) | Ten ideas from your real month, the one true thing each says, five you will never post | You want to post but have "nothing to say yet" |
| [Learning Post](skills/glink-learning-post/SKILL.md) | One post in What? So what? Now what? order, first line inside the moment | You went to an employer talk or finished a course and want to say something real |
| [Project Post](skills/glink-project-post/SKILL.md) | One post on one project moment, first person, optional carousel outline in text | You have a project case and people will not open Featured |
| [Milestone Post](skills/glink-milestone-post/SKILL.md) | Short post for an offer, placement or degree, bottom line first, thanks only with a yes | You got the offer and the usual announcement post makes you wince |
| [Voice Check](skills/glink-voice-check/SKILL.md) | Draft checked against your voice guide, AI tells flagged, claims matched to evidence, every change shown | You used AI on a draft and need to know it still sounds like you |

### 5 · Comment and connect

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Follow List](skills/glink-follow-list/SKILL.md) | Up to 20 employers, alumni and bodies you chose by hand, why each, what to watch for | Your feed is noise and nothing on it relates to the job you want |
| [Thoughtful Comment](skills/glink-thoughtful-comment/SKILL.md) | Two or three angles you could genuinely add to one post, the facts you need, no flattery | "Great post!" is all that comes to mind |
| [Connection Request Note](skills/glink-connection-note/SKILL.md) | Short personal note within LinkedIn's limit, no job ask, a follow-up line for later | The default request to someone you met feels like spam |

### 6 · Be found by recruiters and alumni

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Open to Work Settings](skills/glink-open-to-work/SKILL.md) | Which visibility fits your situation, job preferences from your brief, the privacy caveat | You are not sure the green frame helps or looks desperate |
| [Recruiter Reply](skills/glink-recruiter-reply/SKILL.md) | Scam check first, questions to ask, a reply in your words, what to log | A recruiter messaged you and you do not know if it is real |
| [Alumni Search Plan](skills/glink-alumni-search-plan/SKILL.md) | Manual filters on your university page, up to ten alumni you choose, the reason and one question each | Alumni in your target roles exist and you do not know where to start |

### 7 · The weekly 20 minutes

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Weekly LinkedIn Routine](skills/glink-weekly-routine/SKILL.md) | A 20-minute weekly plan, an if-then plan for a rejection week, a stop rule for scrolling | You post three times after a rejection, then disappear for a month |
| [LinkedIn Activity Log](skills/glink-activity-log/SKILL.md) | One row per action, what it led to, notes for the monthly refresh | You cannot remember who you spoke to or which posts started conversations |
| [Monthly Profile Refresh](skills/glink-monthly-refresh/SKILL.md) | New evidence added, profile lines to update, one thing to stop and one to try | Your profile still describes you on the day you made it |

## Example prompts

- "Run glink-experience-inventory. I am in my final year, I worked in a café for two years, I was treasurer of a society and I did a group project on [topic]. Interview me."
- "Here are five job ads for graduate [role] roles. Run glink-target-role-brief and match their words to my inventory."
- "My headline says 'Student at [university]'. Give me three options from my inventory and brief, and show the evidence behind each."
- "Interview me about my dissertation for my Featured section. I am not sure what I am allowed to share."
- "I went to an employer insight day yesterday. Help me plan a learning post, then check my draft against my voice guide."
- "A recruiter I have never heard of messaged me about a role. Here is the message. Is it real, and what should I reply?"

## Quality bar

- Every line is written from your own interview notes or evidence rows; anything without a row is a gap, not a claim.
- Numbers, skills and quotes appear only with a source you could show in an interview; otherwise they become [placeholders] or come out.
- Drafts follow your Personal Voice Guide, with AI tells stripped and every change shown, and you decide what stays.
- Nothing grades, ranks or profiles a person: skills check your profile, your drafts and fit to your brief, never you or the people you contact.
- Confidential, assessed or other people's work is checked for permission first; legal points end with "check with a qualified adviser".
- You did the work; Claude helps you say it: it interviews you about what really happened and never adds a project, a number, a skill or a quote you cannot back up, it never touches your account (you write, post, comment and connect yourself, with no automation and no scraping), and it never sits a test, interview or assessment for you or writes assessed work unless your university's rules allow it and you say so.

## Where it comes from

The pack is research-informed: LinkedIn's own help pages and policies, UK careers guidance, an AI fluency course for students, published reflection and planning methods, and a scan of what students and graduates say they struggle with on LinkedIn. Sources, limits and the source-to-skill map are in `resources/evidence-and-sources.md`. Cited sources do not imply endorsement. This is a practice resource, not a validated intervention.
