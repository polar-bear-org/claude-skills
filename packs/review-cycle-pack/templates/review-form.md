# Review forms

*Blank template. `review-form-builder` fills this in with you. Add it to your
Project knowledge now, blank, and replace it with the filled version when it
exists.*

*Two forms in one file, 6 to 8 questions each. Hard cap at 8: every question
past that lowers the honesty of all of them.*

**Transparency rule:** everything on both forms is written to be shared with
the person being reviewed. There are no manager-only questions and no hidden
sections. Whatever cannot be said to someone's face does not belong in their
review.

---
