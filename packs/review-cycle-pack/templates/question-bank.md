# 1:1 question bank

*100 questions. This file ships complete: `one-on-one-companion` reads it and
picks two or three for each conversation, chosen for the person and the moment.
Add it to your Project knowledge as it is, and add your own over time. The ones
that work at your company belong here.*

*How it gets used: a checkpoint week gets a growth question, a heavy delivery
week gets a load question, a quiet stretch gets a bigger-picture one. Never the
same questions two weeks running.*

---

## The week in work

1. What did you spend most of your time on this week, and was that the right place for it?
2. What went better than you expected? What went worse?
3. Is anything stuck that I could unstick?
4. What is on your plate right now that shouldn't be?
5. What did you decide this week without checking with anyone? *(A good sign, and worth naming out loud.)*
6. What is the one thing that has to go right next week?
7. Where did you spend time that produced nothing? *(Ask about the process, not the person.)*
8. What did you say no to this week?
9. Is there a decision waiting on me?
10. What would have made this week easier?

## Load and capacity

*For a heavy delivery week, or when someone looks tired.*

11. On a normal week, how full is your plate right now?
12. What would you drop if you could drop one thing?
13. Is the pace this week a one-off, or has it been like this for a while?
14. What is taking longer than it should, and why?
15. Are you working evenings? *(Ask it plainly. The answer is data, not a confession.)*
16. When did you last have a day with no meetings?
17. What are you doing that somebody else should be doing?
18. If two of your projects slipped a week, which two would you pick?
19. How much of your week goes to work nobody asked for but somebody needs?
20. Do you have enough time to do this well, or only enough to do it?

## Growth

*For a checkpoint week, or any time growth is the point of the conversation.*

21. Where do you feel yourself getting better, and where do you feel stuck?
22. What did you do this month that you couldn't have done a year ago?
23. What part of the work do you want more of? What do you want less of?
24. Looking at the growth matrix, which cell in the next column feels closest?
25. What would you need to stop checking with me about?
26. Who here do you learn the most from, and what would make that happen more often?
27. What is the hardest thing you have worked on this year?
28. What skill would change the most about your work if you had it?
29. What are you avoiding because you are not good at it yet?
30. Where do you think you are ahead of where I see you? *(Sit with the answer before responding.)*
31. What feedback have you received more than once in your career?
32. What would you want to be known for on this team?
33. What is the next thing you want to own end to end?
34. Which part of your role do you find genuinely interesting, and which part is just the job?

## Checkpoints

*When a growth memo exists and a 90-day checkpoint is coming due.*

35. Where did the checkpoint land? *(Ask before offering your own read.)*
36. What made it easier or harder than we expected when we set it?
37. Is the checkpoint still the right one, or did the work move?
38. What do you need from me before the next one?
39. What did you learn from it that we didn't plan for?
40. If we set this one again, what would we word differently?

## The bigger picture

*For a quiet stretch, when there is room for a longer conversation.*

41. If you were still here in two years, what would you want to be doing?
42. What part of the company direction interests you most, and where do you see yourself in it?
43. What is the most useful thing you learned this quarter, from anywhere?
44. What would you change about how our team works, if it were up to you?
45. What are we not talking about that we should be?
46. What are we doing today that you think we will have stopped doing in a year?
47. What does our best work look like, and how often do we actually do it?
48. If you ran this company for a week, what would you change first?
49. What is the most common thing clients misunderstand about us?
50. Who outside our team should we be learning from?

## Working together

51. What do I do that helps you, and what do I do that gets in the way?
52. Are you getting enough of my time, too much, or about right?
53. Is there feedback you have been sitting on?
54. When you disagreed with me recently, did you say so? *(If no, that is the thing to work on, and it is the manager's problem to fix.)*
55. How do you want me to handle it when I think you are wrong?
56. What do you wish I asked you about more often?
57. Is this 1:1 useful, and what would make it more useful?
58. What context do you keep having to ask for that I should just be giving you?
59. What do you think my priorities are right now? *(A mismatch here explains a lot.)*
60. Is there anything I promised you that I have not delivered?

## Feedback, given and received

61. Who on the team has helped you this month, and have you told them?
62. Is there someone whose work you would want to learn from more closely?
63. What feedback would you give this team if it changed nothing about your standing?
64. Has anyone given you feedback recently that you disagreed with?
65. What do you think your reputation is here, and is it accurate?
66. Where would a more experienced person have done this differently?

## Clients and the work itself

67. Which piece of client work are you proudest of right now?
68. What is the client not saying that you can feel?
69. Where are we over-servicing, and where are we under-delivering?
70. What would you do differently if this client came back tomorrow with the same brief?
71. What part of our delivery process gets in the way of good work?
72. When did you last get to do the work you are actually best at?

## Working with AI

*Ask these as craft questions, not compliance questions.*

73. Where has AI genuinely made your work better this month, and where has it just made it faster?
74. What have you stopped doing by hand that you would not go back to?
75. Where did AI produce something that looked right and wasn't?
76. What part of your craft would you never hand over, and why?
77. What could our team automate that we are still doing manually?

## Team and collaboration

78. Where is the handoff between you and someone else costing us time?
79. Who do you need something from that you are not getting?
80. Is there a conversation between two people on this team that isn't happening?
81. What do new joiners get wrong about how we work, over and over?
82. Which meeting could we delete without anyone noticing?

## Motivation and energy

83. What was the best part of the last month for you?
84. When did you last lose track of time at work?
85. What drains you that you have not mentioned because it feels small?
86. Are you bored anywhere? *(Boredom is a retention risk that people rarely volunteer.)*
87. What would make you excited to come back after a holiday?
88. If nothing changed here for a year, how would you feel about that?

## When someone is stuck

89. What have you already tried?
90. What would you do if you knew nobody would question it?
91. Is this stuck on a decision, on information, or on someone else?
92. What is the smallest next step you could take this week?
93. Who has solved something like this before?

## After something went wrong

94. Walk me through what happened, from your side.
95. What did you know at the time, and what do you know now?
96. What would you need for it to go differently next time?
97. Is there anything about how we work that made this more likely?

## A new report, or a first 1:1

98. How do you like to be managed? What has worked with past managers, and what hasn't?
99. How do you want feedback: in the moment, or saved for our 1:1?
100. What do you want me to know about how you work best, and what are you hoping to get out of this year?

---

## Questions to avoid

- Anything that asks a person to rate themselves on a number.
- Anything you would not want them to see written down. Every question here
  passes the face test.
- Health, family, and personal life. If someone brings it up, listen as a human
  and keep it out of the notes.
- Conduct, legal or safety matters. Those are not 1:1 prep material: stop, and
  take them to the founder and to professional advice.
