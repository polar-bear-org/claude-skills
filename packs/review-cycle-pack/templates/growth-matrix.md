## How to read this

Levels describe scope of trust, not worth. Nobody is expected to tick every
cell. Moving up means consistently showing the next column, not having a good
month. A strong performer at level 1 still reads as level 1 here: the matrix
measures scope, not how well someone is doing this quarter.

## What this is not

Not a pay grid. Not a promotion promise. Not a performance rating. Promotions
and pay are decisions people make, using this as one input among several.

*A v1 matrix is for growth conversations. Before promotions and salaries hang
on it, it deserves a real calibration round with the managers, which is a
different exercise.*
