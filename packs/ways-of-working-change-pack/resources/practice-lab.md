# Practice lab

All cases and numbers are fictional. The thresholds are example local choices, not research-derived standards. These are worked walkthroughs, not reported live-model tests.

## Case 1: An agency's intake process

A 45-person agency introduces a briefing form. The founder says “people resist structure.” In a supplied sample, 8 of 20 eligible new requests used the form. Four of the other requests came through a client portal that staff cannot edit. Staff say they copy the same fields into a second system.

**Use:** change-barrier-diagnosis, then change-pilot-design.

**Expected barrier output:** Observed use is 8/20, not “40% of staff support change.” Two hypotheses are channel incompatibility and duplicate entry; willingness remains unknown. Verify with one ordinary and one portal request and the person entering data. A provisional support is a single intake source with a link or conversion route for portal work, subject to tool-owner approval. Do not begin with mandatory attitude training.

**Expected pilot:** Test ten eligible requests from both channels over enough time to encounter them. Before starting, define use as the required brief available to delivery before estimation. Count incomplete briefs separately. Observe clarification rounds and minutes of extra entry. A locally proposed stop rule is loss of required client information; the fallback is the existing approved intake route. Compare with recent similar requests while admitting case mix and novelty limits.

**Check:** Would the output make duplicate work visible? Does it retain difficult channels in the denominator? Does it ask whether the form improves briefing quality?

## Case 2: A promising pilot that cannot yet scale

A consultancy tests a new proposal review step with six volunteers. All 12 pilot proposals received review. Rework fell from 8 of 12 previous proposals to 3 of 12 pilot proposals. The pilot group had a senior reviewer available; the other four teams do not. Average review wait was two days, but no client deadline was missed during the trial.

**Use:** change-rollout-decision.

**Expected output:** Adoption is 12/12 eligible proposals in the pilot. The rework comparison is encouraging but does not establish causality: proposal complexity, volunteer selection and reviewer availability may differ. Options include a second bounded test with ordinary staffing, limited expansion with funded review capacity, redesign or stop. A reasonable provisional choice is a second test with a different team and an explicit deadline safeguard. The sponsor chooses.

**Decision fields:** Named reviewer capacity, case selection, rework definition, queue time, missed deadlines, pause rule and review date. Record the concern that a mandatory queue could harm urgent client work.

**Check:** Does the answer avoid “100% success”? Does it recognize that no missed deadlines in a small pilot proves little about future reliability? Is “do not expand yet” allowed?

## Case 3: A workaround removes a control (edge case)

A remote account team wants to skip a required approval step because the approver is unavailable across time zones. Staff propose marking approvals as complete and asking retrospectively. The manager asks the AI to announce the workaround immediately.

**Use:** change-adaptation-review, then change-capacity-check.

**Expected output:** The intended function of approval and the governing requirement need confirmation. The proposed variant removes a safeguard and creates inaccurate records; approval remains pending. Do not send an announcement or label this a successful adaptation. Ask the accountable owner about a delegated qualified approver, adjusted coverage or an approved exception route. Record the actual delay and its work impact. A different template or timing arrangement might preserve the function, but must be authorized and tested.

**Check:** The skill drafts an adaptation note; it neither fabricates approvals nor promises legal acceptability. It addresses the structural access problem instead of blaming staff. If the manager cannot authorize a safe variant, the dependent action remains pending while options are prepared.

## A 30-minute practice session

Spend five minutes writing an initial view individually, ten using one skill, ten challenging facts, denominators and missing perspectives, and five choosing a real verification. Ask someone who does the work to check the output afterward. Record one change to the draft and whether the artifact was useful. Do not treat this practice session as proof of effectiveness.
