# How to Handle Disagreements

**10 practical AI skills for founders and managers who need to work through a specific disagreement and move forward fairly.** Built for agencies and professional-services teams of roughly 20–200.

Separate contested facts from priorities, hear the other view, make a clear objection, explore trade-offs and close a decision without pretending everyone agrees. Use this for one bounded issue; persistent stakeholder friction may need a broader relationship review.

## Start in five minutes

1. Choose a skill below. If unsure, begin with `disagreement-issue-map`.
2. Paste the corresponding file from `portable/` into a chat.
3. Describe your own view, the other person’s actual words, the deadline and who decides. Use aliases.
4. Check the draft, have the real conversation, and bring back corrections.

Example: “Delivery wants two more days of testing; sales promised a Friday launch. Run disagreement-issue-map. I lead delivery and the account director decides the client commitment.”

## Skills

| Skill | When to use it | You leave with |
|---|---|---|
| [Map the Disagreement](skills/disagreement-issue-map/SKILL.md) | What are we actually disagreeing about. | A one-page map distinguishing evidence, predictions, values, process and personal harm. |
| [Test the Contested Facts](skills/disagreement-fact-check/SKILL.md) | We keep arguing about whose numbers are right. | A shared evidence-check plan and a provisional conclusion. |
| [Understand Their View](skills/disagreement-listen-and-check/SKILL.md) | Help me understand why they disagree. | A short listening guide and a checked-understanding note. |
| [State a Constructive Objection](skills/disagreement-clear-dissent/SKILL.md) | I disagree but do not want to make it personal. | A clear objection, alternative and decision request. |
| [Agree How to Resolve It](skills/disagreement-fair-process/SKILL.md) | We cannot even agree how to decide. | A fair participation and decision-process agreement. |
| [Bring Missing Views Into the Room](skills/disagreement-surface-dissent/SKILL.md) | Everyone agrees too quickly. | A bounded dissent session with an evidence and objection log. |
| [Find Workable Trade-offs](skills/disagreement-integrative-options/SKILL.md) | We are stuck between two opposing positions. | An option comparison that tests joint gains and real trade-offs. |
| [Reset an Escalating Exchange](skills/disagreement-reset-exchange/SKILL.md) | This conversation is getting heated. | A pause sentence, revised opening and safe continuation plan. |
| [Close Without False Consensus](skills/disagreement-close-decision/SKILL.md) | We need to move forward although we disagree. | A decision record preserving dissent and implementation responsibilities. |
| [Check Whether It Worked](skills/disagreement-review-resolution/SKILL.md) | The disagreement is settled but i am not sure it helped. | A short outcome review with one corrective action. |

## Use with your assistant

Individual prompts in `portable/` are complete, readable instructions for text assistants. Paste a selected prompt into a conversation; context limits and model behavior vary. For a project, add `portable/ALL-SKILLS.md` as a source and use `PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.md` as project instructions. If retrieval misses a skill, paste its individual prompt.

For an Agent Skills compatible assistant, copy a selected folder from `skills/` into its supported skills location. Claude Code projects can use `.claude/skills/`. The pack includes a plugin manifest for publication; marketplace availability follows publication. No external installation is necessary for the portable prompts.

## Resources

- [Disagreement canvas](templates/disagreement-canvas.md): reusable preparation note.
- [Decision and review record](templates/decision-record.md): closure and follow-through.

These skills help people prepare. They do not send messages, determine who is right from a one-sided account, or turn allegations of harm into a debate. Humans verify evidence, hear affected people and decide. A good process can preserve disagreement. Research does not establish that more conflict improves performance or that these prompts are effective interventions.
