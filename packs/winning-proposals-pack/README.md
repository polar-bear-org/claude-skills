# Claude for Winning Proposals: 31 Claude Skills

31 Claude skills for winning the work when clients have AI too, from qualifying the lead to the proposal you send yourself. For founders, partners and consultants who pitch for their own work.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/winning-proposals-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/winning-proposals-pack)

## What this is

The pack follows one deal from start to finish. You qualify before you meet, read the client's brief (often written with AI) and prepare the call, turn the call into their problem in their words, shape options and scope, write the proposal and the statement of work, review it and walk the client through it, answer objections with the AI one first, then follow up, help the client decide and learn from the result. Proposals are mostly won or lost in discovery, so the pack spends its first half there.

The red line: Claude prepares you to listen better and write faster; it never invents a reference, a result, a number or a client quote, never joins or records a call without everyone's consent, and you send the proposal yourself.

```
Qualify before you meet -> Read the brief, prepare the call -> Turn the call into their problem -> Options and scope -> The proposal and SOW -> Review it and walk them through -> Objections, the AI one first -> Follow up, decide, learn
```

## The skills

### 1 · Qualify before you meet

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [MEDDIC Qualification](skills/disc-meddic-qualification/SKILL.md) | Known and unknown per letter, gaps turned into call questions, a fit note you decide on | Prospects book calls and show interest, yet no proposal closes |
| [Buying Committee Map](skills/disc-buying-committee-map/SKILL.md) | Roles in the client's decision, what each needs to hear, who you have not met | You pitched to an influencer and the decision maker never saw you |
| [Bid/No-Bid Decision](skills/disc-bid-no-bid/SKILL.md) | Your written criteria, the facts against each, the shortlist check, a go or no-go with its reason | An RFP or an AI-compiled shortlist arrives and you are unsure you can win |

### 2 · Read the brief, prepare the call

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [AI-Written Brief Review](skills/disc-ai-brief-review/SKILL.md) | Decisions the client made, generic text, contradictions, missing constraints, questions only they can answer | The brief reads complete, yet you cannot scope from it |
| [Pre-Call Research Brief](skills/disc-pre-call-brief/SKILL.md) | Public facts with sources, the likely trigger, hypotheses to test, what not to assume | You have 20 minutes before the call and want a point of view |
| [Discovery Call Agenda](skills/disc-discovery-call-agenda/SKILL.md) | A short note sent ahead, the outcomes including "no fit", the consent line, who attends | Calls drift, the wrong people turn up, nobody agreed the end |
| [Discovery Call Questions](skills/disc-discovery-call-questions/SKILL.md) | A one-page guide by SPIN type, the brief's gaps first, decision questions | Discovery collects facts and never reaches cost or who decides |
| [Cost of Inaction](skills/disc-cost-of-inaction/SKILL.md) | What staying put costs in their own figures, timing, what waiting changes | The client likes you but nothing makes this urgent |

### 3 · Turn the call into their problem

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Discovery Call Notes](skills/disc-discovery-call-notes/SKILL.md) | Their words marked verbatim, facts, figures, your inferences, open questions, next step | You took notes in a hurry and the proposal needs their exact words |
| [Discovery Recap Email](skills/disc-discovery-recap-email/SKILL.md) | "Here is what we heard", what matters most, what is unsure, next step, an invitation to correct | You want proof you listened before you write the proposal |
| [Problem Statement](skills/disc-problem-statement/SKILL.md) | Point of view in their words, impact in their figures, why now, out of scope, one How Might We | Your proposals describe your firm, not their problem |
| [Issue Tree](skills/disc-issue-tree/SKILL.md) | Causes that do not overlap, each branch tested or untested, what to check first | The client asked AI for the answer and needs to see where it could be wrong |

### 4 · Options and scope

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Good-Better-Best Options](skills/disc-good-better-best-options/SKILL.md) | Three options from your own price list, what each leaves out, no decoy | A single price gets a yes or no, and too often a no |
| [Fee Structure Choice](skills/disc-fee-structure/SKILL.md) | Fee models compared for this work, where AI speed changes the math, your choice and why | The client asks why they pay for hours when AI makes you faster |
| [MoSCoW Scope](skills/disc-moscow-scope/SKILL.md) | Must, should, could and won't, the won't list written out, what moves if budget changes | The AI brief asked for everything and the price covers part of it |

### 5 · The proposal and SOW

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Consulting Proposal](skills/disc-consulting-proposal/SKILL.md) | A short answer-first proposal from your notes, options, proof, AI use, next step | You need a proposal they can read in five minutes and forward |
| [Win Themes](skills/disc-win-themes/SKILL.md) | Two or three themes with need, discriminator and proof, the AI-shortlist test | The buyer compared firms with AI and every firm sounds the same |
| [Case Study Paragraph](skills/disc-case-study-paragraph/SKILL.md) | A matched paragraph and a short version, permission status, approved results only | You need proof a model cannot generate |
| [AI Use Statement](skills/disc-ai-use-statement/SKILL.md) | Where AI is used, what people own, data handling, what the client gains, what you will not use it for | You wonder whether to tell clients you use AI |
| [Statement of Work](skills/disc-statement-of-work/SKILL.md) | Results, deliverables with acceptance criteria, responsibilities, exclusions, change control | The client said yes and the scope must hold |

### 6 · Review it and walk them through

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Proposal Red Team Review](skills/disc-proposal-red-team/SKILL.md) | The draft read from the client's chair, unsourced claims flagged, an edit list | You are about to send and need a buyer's read |
| [Proposal Walk-Through Deck](skills/disc-proposal-walkthrough-deck/SKILL.md) | Six to ten slides, their problem first, options side by side, speaker notes, a read version | A proposal emailed cold goes silent |
| [Proposal Executive Summary](skills/disc-executive-summary/SKILL.md) | One page: situation, complication, question, recommended option, decision and date | The person who signs never met you |

### 7 · Objections, the AI one first

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [AI Objection Answer](skills/disc-ai-objection-answer/SKILL.md) | What AI does well here said first, what stays with you, an option using their own AI | A client says AI could do this in 10 seconds |
| [Like-for-Like Quote Comparison](skills/disc-quote-comparison/SKILL.md) | Both scopes line by line, what each leaves out, risks, questions for the other firm | You lost on price to a far cheaper AI-first quote |
| [Objection Handling Map](skills/disc-objection-handling/SKILL.md) | The objections at this stage, the question behind each, an honest response, its proof | The same objections come back every deal |
| [Negotiation Plan](skills/disc-negotiation-plan/SKILL.md) | Walk-away point and alternative, what you trade, concession order, the question before any discount | You are about to discount to end the discomfort |

### 8 · Follow up, decide, learn

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Mutual Action Plan](skills/disc-mutual-action-plan/SKILL.md) | Steps to a decision both sides own, an owner and a date per step | The client says yes in the room and the deal stalls |
| [Proposal Follow-Up Emails](skills/disc-proposal-follow-up/SKILL.md) | Two useful follow-ups and a close-the-loop note, drafts you send | A proposal sits unanswered and you will not write "just checking in" |
| [Client Decision Brief](skills/disc-client-decision-brief/SKILL.md) | A neutral page with the options, including doing nothing or doing it with their own AI | Your contact must convince people you will never meet |
| [Win/Loss Review](skills/disc-win-loss-review/SKILL.md) | What was meant to happen, what happened, why, where it was decided, what to change | You lost a pitch and want the next one to be better |

## Example prompts

- "Run disc-ai-brief-review on this brief the client sent. I think they wrote it with AI and I cannot tell what they actually need."
- "Here are my notes from this morning's discovery call. Sort them and draft the recap email in their words. I will send it."
- "Build three options from my price list for this scope, and tell me what each one leaves out."
- "The client said AI could do this in 10 seconds. Help me answer honestly, starting with what AI really can do here."
- "Draft a short consulting proposal from my notes, problem statement and options, with a plain statement of how we use AI."
- "We lost this pitch. Run a win/loss review with what I know and what the client told me."

## Quality bar

- Their words stay theirs: anything presented as the client's words is marked verbatim and comes from your notes or a transcript made with everyone's consent.
- Every fact has a source or is marked as an assumption; every number, price, result and reference comes from you.
- Options, not one price, and never a decoy option built to push another.
- The AI question is answered honestly: say what AI does well, never sell against it, never hide your own use.
- Opportunities, briefs, options and drafts are assessed; people are never scored or profiled.
- A named person decides, and you send everything.
- The red line: Claude prepares you to listen better and write faster; it never invents a reference, a result, a number or a client quote, never joins or records a call without everyone's consent, and you send the proposal yourself.
