# Claude Slides for Product Managers: 32 Claude Skills

32 Claude skills for every deck a product person makes, drafted in Claude Slides from your own sources. For product managers, product owners, heads of product, and the people who make decks about the product.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/slides-for-product-managers-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/slides-for-product-managers-pack)

## What this is

The pack follows a product deck from setup to the version people read cold. It starts every deck the same way (your metrics and their sources, a slide design system, a brief with the decider and the ask, a ghost deck of titles), then covers the decks for leaders, the updates for everyone, the team rituals and the decks for customers and the market, and ends with the passes that make a deck hold up. Each skill is one deck type or one deck method, with its inputs, where it breaks, a fixed output template, a finish line and the next skill to run.

Every skill holds one line: Claude Slides drafts the deck from your sources; every number on a slide traces to one, nothing is invented to fill a gap, and you decide what the room is asked to decide.

```
Start Every Deck -> Decks for Leaders -> Updates for Everyone -> Team Decks -> Decks for Customers and the Market -> Make It Hold Up
```

## Install

### In Claude Code

```
/plugin marketplace add polar-bear-org/claude-skills
/plugin install slides-for-product-managers-pack@polar-bear-skills
```

### In Claude.ai

1. Turn on "Code execution and file creation" under Settings > Capabilities.
2. Go to Customize > Skills, click "+", choose "+ Create skill", then "Upload a skill", and upload the skill's zip from the `install/` folder. Repeat for each skill you want.
3. On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner first enables "Cloud code execution and file creation" and "Skills" under Organization settings > Plugins & skills (Policy tab), and can upload skills for the whole organization.

## The skills

### 1 · Start Every Deck

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Deck Context File](skills/deck-context-setup/SKILL.md) | deck-context.md: product, users, metric dictionary with owner, source, base and period, audiences, house words | Every deck starts from zero and Claude guesses your metrics |
| [Slide Design System](skills/deck-design-system/SKILL.md) | Layout library, grid, type scale, colour roles, chart and table styles, components, do and don't rules | Every deck looks different and AI slides ignore your brand |
| [Deck Brief](skills/deck-brief/SKILL.md) | Audience and decider, the one belief or decision, live or read cold, facts with sources, the ask with a date | You cannot get to the point for execs, and decks end with no ask |
| [Ghost Deck Storyline](skills/deck-storyline/SKILL.md) | Action title per slide, evidence per title, executive summary in SCR order, slide budget, titles-only read | Decks eat days of rework and the draft has no argument |
| [Doc to Deck](skills/deck-doc-to-deck/SKILL.md) | Deck drafted from a PRD, PR-FAQ, strategy doc or report, keep-in-the-doc list, doc-versus-deck check | The doc exists and the room still wants slides |
| [Audience Cuts](skills/deck-audience-cuts/SKILL.md) | Exec, sales, company and customer cuts, each with its own first slide and ask, one change log | One update, many audiences, and four files to keep in sync |

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Executive Summary One-Pager](skills/deck-exec-one-pager/SKILL.md) | One slide: bottom line, ask with a date, three reasons with one sourced number each | The exec reads one slide and decides from it |
| [Executive Summary Two-Pager](skills/deck-exec-two-pager/SKILL.md) | Key points and the ask on slide one, sourced insights worth knowing on slide two | One slide loses the story and ten is too many |
| [Roadmap Review Deck](skills/deck-roadmap-review/SKILL.md) | Strategy recap, shipped vs said, Now Next Later with confidence, not doing and why, decision needed | Dates become promises and the loudest leader sets the roadmap |
| [Product Strategy Deck](skills/deck-product-strategy/SKILL.md) | What is and what could be, diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions, what we will not do, endorsement asked | There is no clear strategy to say no against |
| [Quarterly Business Review Deck](skills/deck-qbr/SKILL.md) | Headline, asks first, commitments vs actuals, metrics with base and period, defined RAG, customer variant | QBR prep is days of chasing data and green is not green |
| [Business Case Deck](skills/deck-business-case/SKILL.md) | The ask, cost of doing nothing, options including do nothing, value as ranges, kill criteria | The business case numbers are guesses everyone sees through |
| [Board Deck Product Section](skills/deck-board-slides/SKILL.md) | Three to five pre-read slides, the question for the board, fewest correct metrics, shipped vs planned | The work is invisible to the board and rebuilt every quarter |

### 6 · Make It Hold Up

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Numbers Check](skills/deck-numbers-check/SKILL.md) | Every figure with slide, source, base, period and status, mismatches, draft marks, the chart each figure needs | Made-up or mismatched numbers reach the board |
| [Build in Your Design System](skills/deck-build-in-template/SKILL.md) | Storyline laid out with your layouts and components only, a rule check per slide, exceptions to approve | The content is right but every draft drifts from the brand |
| [One Claim per Slide Restyle](skills/deck-restyle/SKILL.md) | Dense slides split, sentence titles, evidence instead of bullets, AI tells removed, before and after list | The Claude Slides draft is competent but plain and reads as AI |

## Example prompts

- "Run deck-context-setup. Here is our metrics doc and last quarter's QBR. Build the context file and list every metric that has no owner or source."
- "I have 20 minutes with the CPO on Thursday to get two more engineers. Run deck-brief, then deck-storyline, and show me the titles before you draw anything in Claude Slides."
- "Here is the PRD for the new import flow. Run deck-doc-to-deck for the roadmap review and tell me what should stay in the doc."
- "Our roadmap review is next week and sales keeps reading Q3 as a promise. Build the Roadmap Review Deck with a not-on-the-roadmap slide."
- "Run deck-numbers-check on the deck we just drafted. Every figure against the metrics export I pasted, and mark anything unsourced as a draft."
- "Turn this board section into a Pre-Read Slidedoc I can send by share link two days before the meeting."

## Quality bar

- **One method, applied properly.** Each skill uses the real mechanics of its method (the answer-first order, the action titles, the delivery confidence definitions, the Now Next Later horizons), not a generic "gather, analyse, recommend".
- **Story before slides.** The audience, the decider and the ask come first, then a ghost deck of titles, then Claude Slides draws. It tells you where its method breaks and what fits instead.
- **Numbers that hold.** Every figure carries a source, a base and a period. Anything without one stays marked as a draft, and the same figure is checked across slides.
- **Built for Claude Slides as it is.** Claude Slides is in beta (launched 16 September 2026, paid plans). Skills use only what Anthropic has published for it: drafting in a conversation, direct edits, comments on a slide, present, share link, scheduled tasks, doc to deck, and export as PowerPoint or PDF. PowerPoint is an export format here, or a fallback marked as such. Your look comes from written design rules, not from a claim that Slides reads your template.
- **Work is described, people are not graded.** Team decks describe how the work works. Thanks go by name and are never ranked. No slide scores, ranks or profiles a person.
- **The red line:** Claude Slides drafts the deck from your sources; every number on a slide traces to one, nothing is invented to fill a gap, and you decide what the room is asked to decide.

## Where it comes from

The pack is research-informed. The roster comes from the decks product people make every week, month and quarter, and from a scan of what product and project people say they struggle with when the work turns into slides. Each method is attributed to its originator through a public source, and each Claude Slides feature the skills use was checked on an official Anthropic page.
