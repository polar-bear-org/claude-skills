# Stakeholder Relationships Pack

**10 practical AI skills for founders and managers who need to work well with clients, sponsors, partners and other teams over time.** Built for agencies and professional-services teams of roughly 20–200.

Turn repeated friction into a clearer understanding of the work, a realistic commitment, or a boundary people can use. You leave with drafts to discuss and agreements to verify with the people involved.

## Start in five minutes
1. Pick one recurring relationship and one recent example. Use aliases.
2. Choose a skill below and paste its individual portable prompt into a new chat.
3. Describe the work, your authority, what happened and what you need next.
4. Check the draft, get the other person’s perspective, then agree what to try.

Start with **Map the Relationship** when the problem is unclear. Use **Repair a Broken Commitment** when your team caused harm, or **Set Boundaries and Escalate** when the situation calls for protection or a firm limit.

## Skills

| [Build Reliability Through a Small Trial](skills/stakeholder-reliability-trial/SKILL.md) | Good intentions have not improved a stakeholder relationship; create a small commitment people can verify. | Trial card |
| [Repair a Broken Commitment](skills/stakeholder-repair-plan/SKILL.md) | Your team has harmed an ongoing stakeholder relationship; prepare acknowledgment and a credible repair process. | Repair brief |
| [Agree the Working Interface](skills/stakeholder-interface-agreement/SKILL.md) | Repeated friction happens at approvals and handoffs between teams or organizations; agree how the interface works. | One-page interface agreement |

## Use with your assistant
**Portable prompts:** paste any complete file from `portable/` into a text assistant. For a project, add `portable/ALL-SKILLS.md` as a source and use `PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.md`. If retrieval is unreliable, paste the individual prompt directly. Compatibility means readable instructions; behavior varies by assistant.

**Agent Skills:** each `skills/<name>/SKILL.md` folder is standalone. Copy selected folders into an Agent Skills-compatible setup following that product’s instructions. Marketplace availability is pending publication; this review draft makes no live installation claim.

## Judgment stays human
The skills do not assess people’s character, manipulate stakeholders, send messages, or decide formal complaints. A relationship can become workable without becoming friendly. Harm or power abuse may require a safe independent route, not a joint conversation.

The workflows are original practice designs informed by academic research, not validated interventions. Evidence is strongest for general trust, justice and coordination mechanisms; transfer to a particular agency or client relationship needs testing.
