# Evidence and sources

Research checked 10 September 2026. This is a focused research synthesis, not an exhaustive systematic review. The ten workflows are original practice designs; none has been tested as an AI intervention. Academic citations do not imply endorsement.

## What the evidence supports
Trust concerns differ, coordination needs shared information and workable structures, and relationship work has costs. Asking someone for their perspective can add information that imagination alone does not. Repair depends on the harm, the response and the history; no script guarantees forgiveness. These propositions inform practical drafts, not diagnoses of a stakeholder.

## What we designed
The map, six-step procedures, output formats, question limits, trial lengths, review prompts and safety routing are original product choices. They are not named academic instruments. None produces a validated trust score. Two-week or one-cycle trials are practical defaults to adjust, not experimentally established doses.

The package combines several levels of evidence: individual trust experiments, organizational justice studies, and team coordination syntheses. Moving between those levels is a reason for caution, not a license to generalize a numeric effect. We deliberately do not claim effect sizes for the pack.

## Use and evaluation
Before a trial, agree one work outcome and one burden indicator, such as returned briefs and reporting time. At review, ask both parties what changed and record alternative explanations. Do not quietly collect personal ratings or compare employees. A small useful local result is evidence about that trial, not proof of general effectiveness.
