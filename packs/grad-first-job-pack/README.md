# Nail Your First Job with Claude: 31 Claude Skills

31 Claude skills for getting up to speed in your first job, from before day one to your first review. For graduates starting their first job, placement students and anyone starting a new job.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/grad-first-job-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/grad-first-job-pack)

## What this is

The pack follows your first 90 days in order, then the habits that keep you ahead. Before day one you learn the business from public sources, set up one safe place in Claude for your notes and plan the first week. In week one you turn your employer's AI policy into one page and learn what may go into Claude, what you should still do by hand, how to check a draft and how to say you used AI. Then you learn fast (questions, acronyms, who does what, the long documents), agree a 30 60 90 day plan, first goals and ways of working with your manager, and do the everyday work well: emails to senior people, meeting notes, spreadsheets, short reports, presentations and owning a mistake. You keep evidence of progress for your 1:1s and your first review, and you build habits that compound: a weekly learning review, asking for stretch work and meeting people across teams. You cannot beat colleagues on experience yet; you can beat them on speed, care and how well you use AI. Each skill is one named method or one artifact, with the inputs it needs, where it breaks, a fixed output template, a finish line and the next skill to run.

Every skill holds one line: Claude helps you learn the job faster and check your own work; your employer's AI policy comes first, nothing it keeps confidential goes in, Claude never invents what you did, and nothing it drafts goes out in your name until you have read it, checked it and chosen to send it.

```
Before day one -> Week one: AI at work, safely -> Learn fast -> Your first 30, 60, 90 days -> Do the work well -> Feedback and progress -> Habits that compound
```

## Install

### In Claude Code

```
/plugin marketplace add polar-bear-org/claude-skills
/plugin install grad-first-job-pack@polar-bear-skills
```

### In Claude.ai

1. Turn on "Code execution and file creation" under Settings > Capabilities.
2. Go to Customize > Skills, click "+", choose "+ Create skill", then "Upload a skill", and upload the skill's zip from the `install/` folder. Repeat for each skill you want.
3. On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner first enables "Cloud code execution and file creation" and "Skills" under Organization settings > Plugins & skills (Policy tab), and can upload skills for the whole organization.

Every skill works in a plain chat on the Free plan with what you paste in. Check your employer's AI policy before you install anything on a work device or a work account. Some skills name an optional Claude surface: Projects and memory for your first 90 days, Incognito chats for one-off questions, Claude in Chrome (paid plans) for public company research, Claude for Excel for spreadsheets, Claude Docs (beta) for written work, and Claude Slides (beta) or Claude for PowerPoint for presentations.

## The skills

### 1 · Before day one

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Company Research Brief](skills/fjob-company-research-brief/SKILL.md) | One-page brief from public sources, five "so what for my role" lines, ten questions for week one | You start in two weeks and know the logo, not the business |
| [Claude Project Setup](skills/fjob-claude-project-setup/SKILL.md) | Project instructions for 90 days, what goes in and never goes in, which account, Incognito and memory check | You want one place for the first 90 days and do not know what is safe to put there |
| [First Week Plan](skills/fjob-first-week-plan/SKILL.md) | Day-by-day plan, questions for manager, buddy and IT, documents to ask for, end-of-week note | You start Monday and feel anxious about getting the first week wrong |

### 2 · Week one: AI at work, safely

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [AI Policy Card](skills/fjob-ai-policy-card/SKILL.md) | Your AI policy on one page, gaps as questions, a no-policy branch to agree with your manager | You were told to "use AI responsibly" and nobody said what that means here |
| [Data Check Before You Paste](skills/fjob-data-check/SKILL.md) | Green, amber or red for one piece of content, how to strip identifiers, the safer route | You are about to paste a client email, a spreadsheet or a colleague's notes into Claude |
| [AI Task Picker](skills/fjob-ai-task-picker/SKILL.md) | Tasks sorted into do it yourself, with Claude, Claude first pass, not allowed; what you learn by hand | AI could do half your tasks and you worry you will stop learning the job |
| [AI Output Check](skills/fjob-ai-output-check/SKILL.md) | Claim-by-claim check of a Claude draft, what you changed, what still needs a colleague | Claude gave you something that looks finished and it is going to your manager or a client |
| [AI Disclosure Note](skills/fjob-ai-disclosure-note/SKILL.md) | One or two lines on what Claude did and what you did, worded to your policy, and how to say it | Your policy says to disclose AI use, or you feel tempted to hide it |

### 3 · Learn fast

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Question Log](skills/fjob-question-log/SKILL.md) | Running log of questions and answers, batched questions for your check-in, a "stuck, then ask" rule | You have fifty questions and worry you are asking too many |
| [Work Glossary](skills/fjob-work-glossary/SKILL.md) | Acronyms and internal terms in plain words, where you heard them, confirmed or to check | Every meeting is three-letter acronyms and you nod along |
| [Who Does What Map](skills/fjob-who-does-what-map/SKILL.md) | Team and stakeholder map by role, what each owns and needs, who to ask for what | You do not know who decides, who knows and who to copy in |
| [Document Explainer](skills/fjob-document-explainer/SKILL.md) | Plain summary of one shareable document, its terms, what it asks of you, three questions | You were sent a 40-page document and told to "get familiar with it" |

### 4 · Your first 30, 60, 90 days

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [30 60 90 Day Plan](skills/fjob-30-60-90-plan/SKILL.md) | Understand by 30, contribute by 60, own a piece by 90, checkpoints and support, a draft for your manager | You want to show your manager you have a plan, not just turn up |
| [First Goals Draft](skills/fjob-first-goals/SKILL.md) | Three to five proposed goals, SMART where counting is honest, one learning goal, how you will be reviewed | Week two is ending and nobody has written down what success looks like |
| [Ways of Working Note](skills/fjob-ways-of-working-note/SKILL.md) | Questions to agree with your manager and a half-page note of what you both agreed | You cannot tell how your manager wants to hear from you |
| [Weekly Plan](skills/fjob-weekly-plan/SKILL.md) | Three outcomes, tasks by urgent and important, time blocks, what to ask your manager to prioritise | You have tasks from three people and no idea what to do first |
| [Priority Clash Reply](skills/fjob-priority-clash-reply/SKILL.md) | Factual trade-off note with two options and a short message asking someone to choose | Two senior people want something by Friday and you cannot do both |

### 5 · Do the work well

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Email to a Senior Colleague](skills/fjob-email-to-senior/SKILL.md) | Point and ask first, context after, deadline, subject line, tone check | You rewrote a two-line email to a director five times |
| [Meeting Notes and Actions](skills/fjob-meeting-notes/SKILL.md) | Decisions, actions with owner and date, open questions, a follow-up message | You left a meeting unsure what was decided and who does what |
| [Spreadsheet Check](skills/fjob-spreadsheet-check/SKILL.md) | Analysis plan, formulas in plain words, layout check, totals reconciled, what the numbers say | You were handed a spreadsheet and a question and Excel was never taught |
| [Short Report](skills/fjob-short-report/SKILL.md) | One to two pages, answer first, three supporting points, what you are unsure of, the ask | Your manager asked you to "write it up" and you have notes, not a story |
| [Presentation of Your Work](skills/fjob-presentation-of-work/SKILL.md) | Storyline, full-sentence slide headlines with evidence, likely questions, a rehearsal | You have to present your first piece of work to the team or a senior audience |
| [Mistake Recovery Note](skills/fjob-mistake-recovery/SKILL.md) | Facts, impact, fix, an early message to your manager, what changes next time | You made a mistake at work and want to hide it |

### 6 · Feedback and progress

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Brag Document](skills/fjob-brag-document/SKILL.md) | Dated log of what you did and the result, lessons, monthly recap by theme | Something went well this week and you will have forgotten it by your review |
| [Weekly Update to Your Manager](skills/fjob-weekly-update/SKILL.md) | Five lines: done, next, blocked, decisions needed, one thing learned | Your manager says "keep me posted" and you do not know how much to say |
| [One to One Agenda](skills/fjob-one-to-one-agenda/SKILL.md) | Agenda on goals, blockers, decisions, one business question, a feedback ask; notes after | Your 1:1 is tomorrow and you have nothing written down |
| [Feedback Request](skills/fjob-feedback-request/SKILL.md) | Two or three specific questions about one piece of work, a template for what you hear, one change | You hear "it's fine" and want to know what would make it better |
| [First Review Prep](skills/fjob-first-review-prep/SKILL.md) | Evidence against your first goals, a self-review in your words, your questions, support you need | Your probation review is booked and you do not know what to say about yourself |

### 7 · Habits that compound

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Weekly Learning Review](skills/fjob-learning-review/SKILL.md) | 15-minute Friday review, one thing to practise next week, the month's pattern | Weeks blur and you cannot say what you have got better at |
| [Stretch Assignment Ask](skills/fjob-stretch-assignment-ask/SKILL.md) | One or two pieces of work beyond your tasks, what you would learn, how you protect your work, the message | You are on top of the basics and want more than the tasks you were given |
| [Internal Network Plan](skills/fjob-internal-network-plan/SKILL.md) | Six to ten people across teams worth meeting for the work, what to ask and offer, an invite you send | You only know your own team and the work keeps crossing into others |

## Example prompts

- "Run fjob-company-research-brief. I start as a [role] at [company] in two weeks. Use only their public website, annual report and the news from the last year."
- "Here is my company's AI policy. Turn it into an AI Policy Card and list what it does not answer as questions for my manager."
- "I am about to paste this email thread into Claude to draft a reply. Run Data Check Before You Paste first."
- "Here is a summary Claude wrote for my manager. Run AI Output Check claim by claim before I send it."
- "My probation review is in three weeks. Here are my first goals and my brag document. Run First Review Prep and keep it in my words."
- "Two senior colleagues both want their piece by Friday. Here is what I have promised. Draft a Priority Clash Reply that asks them to choose."

## Quality bar

- **Your employer's AI policy comes first.** Every work skill starts by asking what your policy allows and which Claude account you are in, and runs Data Check Before You Paste before anything internal goes in.
- **One method, applied properly.** Each skill uses the real mechanics of its source (the traffic light, SIFT, the 30 60 90 stages, SMART, the urgent and important grid, bottom line up front, the reflective cycle), not a generic "gather, analyse, recommend".
- **Nothing invented.** No made-up achievements, numbers, quotes or results. Anything missing becomes a [placeholder] or a question to ask.
- **Work is reviewed, people are not.** Maps and notes describe roles and work, never judgments of colleagues. Nothing grades or ranks anyone, and no promise that any skill leads to promotion.
- **Legal and data protection points go to a person.** Employment, contract, probation and data protection points end with "check with a qualified adviser" or HR.
- **The red line:** Claude helps you learn the job faster and check your own work; your employer's AI policy comes first, nothing it keeps confidential goes in, Claude never invents what you did, and nothing it drafts goes out in your name until you have read it, checked it and chosen to send it.

## Where it comes from

The pack is research-informed. The roster comes from what new starters actually produce in their first 90 days and from a scan of what graduates and new starters say they struggle with, including unclear AI rules at work. Each method is attributed to its originator through a public source: UK guidance from Acas, gov.uk, the ICO and the NCSC, Anthropic's privacy centre and AI Fluency framework, university careers and reflection toolkits, the Center for Creative Leadership, research papers and originator pages. Sources and their limits are in `resources/evidence-and-sources.md`.

Cited sources do not imply endorsement. This is a practice resource, not a validated intervention.
