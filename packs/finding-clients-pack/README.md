# Claude Guide for Finding Clients: 32 Claude Skills

32 Claude skills to win clients, from your warm network to the signed proposal. For founders and owners who sell their own expertise, independent consultants, fractional executives and partners who sell and deliver.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/finding-clients-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/finding-clients-pack)

## What this is

Winning clients is a part-time job done between delivery days. This guide follows it in order: keep the pipeline full while you deliver, go back to past clients and referrers, work your network and warm introductions, sharpen your positioning and offer, write down your proof, open new accounts only on a real trigger, qualify and run discovery, then write the proposal, set the price and get to a clear yes or no. Each skill is one artifact or one named method, and each one hands over to the next.

The red line: Claude researches, prepares and drafts from your real work and real clients; you choose who to talk to, put it in your own words and send it yourself. Nothing is invented, automated or sent for you.

```
Pipeline -> Past clients and referrals -> Network and introductions -> Positioning and offer -> Proof -> New accounts -> Qualify and discover -> Proposals, price and the yes
```

## The skills

### 1 · Keep the pipeline full while you deliver

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Business Development Plan](skills/win-business-development-plan/SKILL.md) | 90-day goal from your numbers, warm-first channel mix, weekly actions you control, what you stop | One month is full, the next is empty |
| [Weekly Business Development Hour](skills/win-weekly-bd-hour/SKILL.md) | Fixed agenda, follow-ups due, warm signals, people to reconnect with, one conversation to book | Selling stops whenever delivery peaks |
| [Pipeline Review](skills/win-pipeline-review/SKILL.md) | Stages defined by buyer actions, dated next steps, stale deals named, gap to goal | The pipeline looks full but nothing has moved |
| [Win/Loss Review](skills/win-win-loss-review/SKILL.md) | Expected versus actual per deal, what to keep and change, questions for the client | You lost and heard only "we went another way" |

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Past Client Reconnect List](skills/win-past-client-list/SKILL.md) | Past clients and moved contacts, what changed, a reason to write, a short run you pick | You have not spoken to your best past clients in a year |
| [Key Account Plan](skills/win-key-account-plan/SKILL.md) | Client goals in their words, buying roles, relationship gaps, further work that fits, 90 days | A client you rely on could leave with one message |

## Quality bar

- Every fact about a person or organisation comes from a public source, linked, or from your own records; what has no source is dropped or marked unknown.
- Every number is yours: goals, prices, results and costs come from your inputs or stay as [placeholders].
- Nothing grades, ranks or scores people. Organisations and deals are compared against your brief; closeness and fit are set by you.
- Warm before cold: past clients, referrers and your network come first, and cold outreach is one person on one real trigger.
- Legal, contract, tax, referral fee and data protection points are marked "check with a qualified adviser".
- The red line: Claude researches, prepares and drafts from your real work and real clients; you choose who to talk to, put it in your own words and send it yourself. Nothing is invented, automated or sent for you.
